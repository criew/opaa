# Legion

Legion, real name David Haller, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Israel and first appearing in New Mutants #25 (March, 1985). He works as an Adventurer, Student and is affiliated with X-Men and Muir Island X-Men. He has Green / Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Fire Control, Flight, Force Fields, Reality Warping, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 40 for speed, 30 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 40.
