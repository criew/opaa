# Atom (CW)

Atom (CW), real name Ray Palmer, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 3: Episode 1. He works as a CEO, Vigilante. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 95 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Energy Blasts, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 70 for speed, 75 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 13.
