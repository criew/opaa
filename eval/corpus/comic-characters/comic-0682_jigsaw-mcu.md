# Jigsaw (MCU)

Jigsaw (MCU), real name Billy Russo, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Punisher Season 1. He works as a President of Anvil Security, hitman, military contractor. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 82 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 45 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
