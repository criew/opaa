# Wolfsbane

Wolfsbane, real name Rahne Sinclair, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, born in Somewhere in Ross and Cromarty, Scotland and first appearing in Marvel Graphic Novel #4: New Mutants. She works as a Teacher and is affiliated with Excalibur, Weapon X and New Mutants. She has Green eyes and Auburn hair and stands 366 cm tall and weighs 473 kg. Her notable abilities include Enhanced Senses, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 70 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 40 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 4.
