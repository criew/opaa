# The Flash

The Flash, real name Barry Allen, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Fallville, Iowa and first appearing in Showcase #4 (October, 1956). He works as a Forensic Scientist and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha, Blue Lantern Corps, Flash Family, Flash Family, Superfriends, Justice League, Justice League Antarctica and Justice League of America. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 88 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Longevity, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina and Super Speed. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 100 for speed, 60 for durability and 60 for combat, giving an overall score of 16.
