# Nightshade

Nightshade, real name Eve Eden, is a neutral-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Kansas and first appearing in Captain Atom #82 - Captain Atom vs the Ghost. She works as a Princess, Adventurer. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Flight, Intelligence, Magic, Stamina, Stealth and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 65 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
