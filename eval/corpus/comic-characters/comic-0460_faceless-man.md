# Faceless Man

Faceless Man is a neutral-aligned character created by George R. R. Martin, born in Valyria/Braavos and first appearing in A Song of Ice & Fire; Book 1: A Game Of Thrones. They work as an Assassin, Servent of Many-Faced God. Their notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, they score 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 65 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, their overall score is 8.
