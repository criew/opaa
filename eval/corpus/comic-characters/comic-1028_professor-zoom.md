# Professor Zoom

Professor Zoom, real name Eobard Thawne, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Flash #139 (September, 1963). He is affiliated with The Society, League of Assassins, Flash Family and Black Lantern Corps. He has Blue eyes and Strawberry Blond hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Reflexes, Stamina and Super Speed. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 100 for speed, 20 for durability and 20 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 11.
