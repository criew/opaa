# Itachi Uchiha

Itachi Uchiha, real name Uchiha Itachi, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Naruto, Volume #16, Chapter #139. He works as a S-Rank Missing-nin. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 58 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Constructs, Fire Control, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 80 for speed, 75 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 25.
