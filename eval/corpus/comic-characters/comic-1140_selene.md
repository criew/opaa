# Selene

Selene, real name Selene, is a good-aligned female Vampire character created by IDW Publishing, born in Hungary and first appearing in Underworld #1 - Underworld. She works as a Death Dealer,Vampire Elder. She has Blue eyes and Brown / White hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 65 for speed, 75 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 10.
