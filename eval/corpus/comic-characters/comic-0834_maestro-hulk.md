# Maestro Hulk

Maestro Hulk, real name Bruce Banner, is a bad-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, born in Dayton, Ohio and first appearing in Incredible Hulk: Future Imperfect #1. He works as a Nuclear physicist, Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D. He has Green eyes and White hair and stands 274 cm tall and weighs 900 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 85 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 35.
