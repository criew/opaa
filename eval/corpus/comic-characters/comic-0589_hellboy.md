# Hellboy

Hellboy, real name Anung Un Rama, is a good-aligned male Demon character created by Dark Horse Comics, born in East Bromwich, England and first appearing in Dime Press #4 (May 1, 1993). He works as a Former BPRD Agent, Monster Hunter, Paranormal Investigator and is affiliated with Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense. He has Orange eyes and Black hair and stands 211 cm tall and weighs 178 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 50 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 28.
