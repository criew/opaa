# Poison Ivy

Poison Ivy, real name Pamela Isley, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Seattle, Washington and first appearing in Batman #181. She works as a Criminal, Botanist and is affiliated with The Society and Birds of Prey. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 70 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
