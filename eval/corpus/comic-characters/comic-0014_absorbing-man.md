# Absorbing Man

Absorbing Man, real name Carl Creel, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York City, New York and first appearing in Journey Into Mystery (1952) Issue #114. He works as a Professional criminal; former professional boxer and is affiliated with Marvel Knights, Masters of Evil, National Force, Secret Empire and Giants. He has Blue eyes and is bald and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 122 kg. His notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability, Energy Absorption, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Invulnerability, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Size Changing and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 25 for speed, 100 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
