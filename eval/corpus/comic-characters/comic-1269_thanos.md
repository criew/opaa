# Thanos

Thanos, real name Thanos, is a bad-aligned male Eternal character created by Marvel Comics, born in Titan and first appearing in Iron Man #55. He works as a Conqueror, worshiper of Death and is affiliated with Masters of Evil, Gods, Titans, Galactic Council, Infinity Watch and Defenders. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 201 cm tall and weighs 443 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Magic, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 75 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 77.
