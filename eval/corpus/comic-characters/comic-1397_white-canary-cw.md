# White Canary (CW)

White Canary (CW), real name Sara Lance, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Metropolis and first appearing in Arrow season 1. She works as a Vigilante. She has Blue / White eyes and Yellow hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 100 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 11.
