# Aquaman

Aquaman, real name Orin, is a good-aligned male Atlantean character created by DC Comics, born in Atlantis and first appearing in More Fun Comics #73 (November, 1941). He works as a Protector of the Seas and Oceans, King of Poseidonis and is affiliated with Aquaman Family, Justice League Atlantis, Superfriends, Justice League Europe, Justice League Antarctica, Justice League, Black Lantern Corps and Justice League of America. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 146 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 80 for speed, 80 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 14.
