# Tobirama Senju

Tobirama Senju, real name Senju Tobirama, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Naruto: Volume 14, Chapter 118. He works as a Hokage, Leader of Team Tobirama. He has Red eyes and White hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 70 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Electrokinesis, Energy Absorption, Energy Constructs, Fire Control, Force Fields, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Teleportation and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 100 for speed, 75 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 17.
