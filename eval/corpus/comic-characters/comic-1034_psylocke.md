# Psylocke

Psylocke, real name Elizabeth Braddock, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Braddock Manor, England and first appearing in Captain Britain #8 (December, 1976) / New Mutants Annual #2 (October, 1986) (US). She works as an Adventurer, formerly a STRIKE operative, fashion model and is affiliated with X-Men, Apocalypse's Horsemen, X-Force, Ultimate X-Men and Four Horsemen of Apocalypse. She has Blue eyes and Purple hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 70 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Illusions, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Telepathy Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
