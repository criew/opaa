# YellowJacket (MCU)

YellowJacket (MCU), real name Darren Cross, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in United States of America and first appearing in Ant-Man (2015). He works as a CEO of Cross Technological Enterprises. He has Green eyes and No Hair hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Flight, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Strength and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 60 for speed, 70 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
