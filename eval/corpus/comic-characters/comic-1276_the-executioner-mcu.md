# The Executioner (MCU)

The Executioner (MCU), real name Skurge, is a neutral-aligned male Asgardian character created by Marvel Comics, born in Asgard and first appearing in Thor: Ragnarok. He works as a Soldier. He has Hazel eyes and is bald and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 92 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 60 for speed, 75 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
