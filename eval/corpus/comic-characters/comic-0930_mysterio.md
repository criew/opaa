# Mysterio

Mysterio, real name Quentin Beck, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York, New York and first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #13 (1964). He works as a Criminal; former Hollywood special effects designer and stunt man and is affiliated with Sinister Six, The FIRM, X-Terminators and A.R.M.O.R. He has Brown eyes and is bald and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Illusions, Intelligence, Jump, Magic, Peak Human Condition, Stamina, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 15 for speed, 40 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
