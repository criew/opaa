# Animal Man

Animal Man, real name Bernhard Baker, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Strange Adventures #180 (September, 1965). He is affiliated with Justice League Europe, Justice League of America and Time Masters. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 45 for speed, 85 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
