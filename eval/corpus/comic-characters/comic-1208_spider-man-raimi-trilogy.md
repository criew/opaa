# Spider-Man (Raimi Trilogy)

Spider-Man (Raimi Trilogy), real name Peter Parker, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Sony Pictures, first appearing in Spider-Man. He works as a Hero, Pizza Delivery Man, Photographer. He has Grey eyes and Brown hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 70 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Jump, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina and Stealth.
