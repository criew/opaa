# Devastator

Devastator, real name Bruce Wayne, is a bad-aligned male Mutant character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City (Earth -1) and first appearing in Dark Days: The Casting #1. He works as a Conqueror, Serial Killer, former Businessman, Adventurer. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 244 cm tall and weighs 412 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Energy Absorption, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 70 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 22.
