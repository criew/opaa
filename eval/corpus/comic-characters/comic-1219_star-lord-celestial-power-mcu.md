# Star-Lord (Celestial Power MCU)

Star-Lord (Celestial Power MCU), real name Peter Quill, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in St. Charles, Missouri, United States of America and first appearing in Guardians of the Galaxy (2014). He works as a Mercenary, Adventurer. He has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 70 for strength, 65 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
