# Spider-Man Noir

Spider-Man Noir, real name Peter Parker, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Queens, New York City, New York and first appearing in Spider-Man Noir #1 (February, 2009). He works as an Adventurer, reporter, photographer, vigilante and is affiliated with New Justice Team and Spider-Army. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 78 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 70 for speed, 75 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
