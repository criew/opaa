# Namorita

Namorita, real name Namorita Prentiss, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, born in Atlantis Outskirts and first appearing in SUB-MARINER Vol. 2 #50 (1972). She works as a Superhero, formerly Leader of Atlantis and is affiliated with Avengers Resistance, Defenders, Secret Defenders, Atlanteans and New Warriors. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Flight, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 70 for strength, 45 for speed, 70 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
