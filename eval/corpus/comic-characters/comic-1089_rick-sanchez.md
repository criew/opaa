# Rick Sanchez

Rick Sanchez, real name Rick Sanchez, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by Cartoon Network, born in Earth (Dimension C-137) and first appearing in Rick and Morty #1 - The Wubba Lubba Dub Dub of Wall Street. He works as a Scientist, Inventor, Arms salesman and is affiliated with Flex Fighters. He has Brown eyes and Blue hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 62 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Energy Beams, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stealth, Teleportation, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 55 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 19.
