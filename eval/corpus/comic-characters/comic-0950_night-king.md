# Night King

Night King is a bad-aligned male character created by George R. R. Martin, born in Likely Land of Always Winter and first appearing in Game of Thrones; Season 4: Oathkeeper. He works as a Leader of the White Walkers. He has Blue eyes and stands 175 cm tall. His notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability, Fire Resistance, Intelligence, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 30 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 13.
