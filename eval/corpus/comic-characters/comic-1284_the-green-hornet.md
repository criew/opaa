# The Green Hornet

The Green Hornet, real name Britt Reid, is a good-aligned male Human character, born in Chicago and first appearing in The Green Hornet radio program (January 31, 1936). He works as a Fake Criminal, Vigilante, Playboy Newspaper Publisher. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 92 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Fire Control, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 45 for strength, 30 for speed, 45 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
