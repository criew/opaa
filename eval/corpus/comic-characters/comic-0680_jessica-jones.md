# Jessica Jones

Jessica Jones, real name Jessica Campbell Jones Cage, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Forrest Hills, New York City and first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #4 (September, 1963). She works as a Special Consultant to "The Pulse", a Daily Bugle supplement; formerly private investigator, adventurer, short-time vigilante and is affiliated with Marvel Knights, The Ultimates, The Six, Defenders, Defenders and A-Force. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 56 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Flight, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 45 for strength, 50 for speed, 70 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 6.
