# Livewire

Livewire, real name Leslie Willis, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Metropolis and first appearing in Action Comics #835 (March, 2006). She works as a Reporter and is affiliated with The Society. She has Blue eyes and Blue hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. Her notable abilities include Electrokinesis, Element Control and Energy Absorption. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 90 for speed, 95 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 9.
