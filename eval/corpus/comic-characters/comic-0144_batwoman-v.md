# Batwoman V

Batwoman V, real name Katherine Rebecca Kane, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in 52 #7 (June, 2006). She is affiliated with Batman Family and Female Furies. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 178 cm tall. Her notable abilities include Dexterity, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 30 for speed, 25 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
