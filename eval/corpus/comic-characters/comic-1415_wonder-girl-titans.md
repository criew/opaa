# Wonder Girl (Titans)

Wonder Girl (Titans), real name Donna Troy, is a good-aligned female Amazon character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Titans Season 1: Episode 8. She works as a Photographer, Vigilante. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 60 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 65 for speed, 65 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 15.
