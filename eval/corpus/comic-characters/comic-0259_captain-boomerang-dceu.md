# Captain Boomerang (DCEU)

Captain Boomerang (DCEU), real name Digger Harkness, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Korumburra, Australia and first appearing in Suicide Squad. He works as a Professional Criminal. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 92 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
