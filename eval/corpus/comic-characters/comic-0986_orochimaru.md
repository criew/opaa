# Orochimaru

Orochimaru is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Volume #5, Naruto Chapter #45. He works as a Sound Village Ninja, Leaf Village Ninja, Founder and Leader of Otogakure Scientis. He has Hazel eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Fire Control, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 60 for speed, 65 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 19.
