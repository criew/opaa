# Misty Knight

Misty Knight, real name Mercedes 'Misty' Knight, is a good-aligned female Cyborg character created by Marvel Comics, born in Place of birth unknown and first appearing in Tales from the Age of Apocalypse: Sinister Bloodlines. She works as a Private investigator; Formerly policewoman and is affiliated with Heroes For Hire and Daughters of the Dragon. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Energy Blasts, Force Fields, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 25 for speed, 45 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
