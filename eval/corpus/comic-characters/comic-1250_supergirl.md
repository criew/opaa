# Supergirl

Supergirl, real name Kara Zor-El, is a good-aligned female Kryptonian character created by DC Comics, born in Argo City, Krypton and first appearing in Superman/Batman #8 (May, 2004). She works as an Intern, Adventurer, Student, Crime-Fighter and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha, Teen Titans, Female Furies, Legion of Super-Heroes, Justice League and Red Lantern Corps. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Energy Absorption, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 19.
