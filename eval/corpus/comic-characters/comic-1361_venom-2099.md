# Venom 2099

Venom 2099, real name Kron Stone, is a bad-aligned male Symbiote character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Punisher 2099 #1. He works as a Mass murderer. He has White eyes and No Hair hair and stands 178 cm tall. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 40 for speed, 65 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
