# Vixen (CW)

Vixen (CW), real name Amaya Jiwe, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Zambesi and first appearing in DC's Legends of Tomorrow Season 2: Episode 1. She works as a Protector of Zambesi. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 65 for speed, 55 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
