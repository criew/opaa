# Killian (MCU)

Killian (MCU), real name Aldrich Killian, is a bad-aligned male Metahuman character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Iron Man 3. He works as a Businessman, Conspirator, Terrorist. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Energy Blasts, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 60 for speed, 70 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
