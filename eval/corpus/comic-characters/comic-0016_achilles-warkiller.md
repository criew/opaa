# Achilles Warkiller

Achilles Warkiller, real name Achilles Warkiller, is a good-aligned male God / Eternal character created by DC Comics, born in Thalarion and first appearing in Wonder Woman Vol 3 #30. He works as an Adventurer, Warrior, King. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 88 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 75 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 19.
