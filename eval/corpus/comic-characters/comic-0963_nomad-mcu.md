# Nomad (MCU)

Nomad (MCU), real name Steve Rogers, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Brooklyn, New York and first appearing in Captain America: First Vengeance #1. He works as an Adventurer, Soldier. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 108 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 60 for speed, 65 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
