# Banshee

Banshee, real name Sean Cassidy, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Cassidy Keep, Ireland and first appearing in X-Men #28 (January, 1967). He works as a Currently founder and director of operation for X-Corps, formerly Adventurer and Headmaster at the Massachusetts Academy, in charge of Generation X, Interpol Inspector and Agent, freelance detective, professional criminal, involuntary operative for Factor and is affiliated with X-Corps, Generation X, Cerebro's X-Men, Apocalypse's Horsemen, X-Men and Factor Three. He has Green eyes and Strawberry Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Enhanced Hearing, Flight and Force Fields. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 60 for speed, 40 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
