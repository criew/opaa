# Tomar-Re

Tomar-Re, real name Tomar-Re, is a good-aligned male character created by DC Comics, born in Xudar and first appearing in Green Lantern Vol 2 #6. He works as a Scientist. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 95 kg. His notable abilities include Element Control, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Matter Manipulation and Super Speed. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 100 for speed, 75 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 21.
