# Deathstroke (Injustice)

Deathstroke (Injustice), real name Slade Wilson, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Injustice: Gods Among us (November, 2013). He works as a Mercenary, Assassin, Bounty Hunter, Hit Man. He has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 70 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
