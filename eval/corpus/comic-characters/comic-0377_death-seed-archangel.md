# Death Seed Archangel

Death Seed Archangel, real name Warren Worthington, is a neutral-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Centerport, Long Island, New York and first appearing in The X-Men #1. He works as an adventurer. He has Red eyes and No Hair hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 90 for speed, 80 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 15.
