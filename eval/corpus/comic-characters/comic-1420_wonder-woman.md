# Wonder Woman

Wonder Woman, real name Diana Prince, is a good-aligned female Amazon character created by DC Comics, born in Themyscira and first appearing in All-Star Comics #8 (December, 1941). She works as an Adventurer, Emissary to the world of Man, Protector of Paradise Island; former Goddess of Truth and is affiliated with Superfriends, Justice League Elite, All-Star Squadron, Justice League Antarctica, Black Lantern Corps, Justice Society of America, Justice League and Justice League of America. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 74 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 80 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 17.
