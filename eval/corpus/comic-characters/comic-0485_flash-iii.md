# Flash III

Flash III, real name Wally West, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Blue Valley, Nebraska and first appearing in Flash #110 (January, 1960). He works as an Adventurer, Mechanic for the KCPD and is affiliated with Flash Family, Titans, Teen Titans, Justice League Elite, Justice League Europe, Teen Titans and Justice League. He stands 183 cm tall and weighs 86 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Dimensional Travel, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Longevity, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 100 for speed, 60 for durability and 30 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
