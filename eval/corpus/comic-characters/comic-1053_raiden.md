# Raiden

Raiden, real name Jack, is a good-aligned male Human / Altered character, born in Liberia and first appearing in Metal Gear Solid 2: Sons of Liberty. He works as a Child soldier, Special forces soldier, Spy, Mercenary. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 78 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 65 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 30.
