# X-23

X-23, real name Laura Kinney, is a good-aligned female Mutant / Clone character created by Marvel Comics, born in The Facility, location unrevealed and first appearing in NYX #3. She works as an Adventurer, Student, former; Assassin, Prostitute and is affiliated with Apocalypse's Horsemen, New X-Men, X-Force and X-Men. She has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 155 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 25 for strength, 40 for speed, 100 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
