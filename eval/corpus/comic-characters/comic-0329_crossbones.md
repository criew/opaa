# Crossbones

Crossbones, real name Brock Rumlow, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in America and first appearing in Captain America #360. He works as a Mercenary and is affiliated with Alpha Primitives, HYDRA and Thunderbolts. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 131 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 45 for speed, 40 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
