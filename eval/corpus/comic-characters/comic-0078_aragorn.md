# Aragorn

Aragorn, real name Aragorn II Elessar, is a good-aligned male Human character created by J. R. R. Tolkien, born in Eriador. He works as a Chieftain of the Dúnedain, King of the Reunited Kingdom and is affiliated with Fellowship of the Ring. He has Grey eyes and Brown hair and stands 198 cm tall. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 40 for speed, 55 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
