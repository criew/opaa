# Killer Croc (DCEU)

Killer Croc (DCEU), real name Waylon Jones, is a bad-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Suicide Squad. He has Brown eyes and No Hair hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 119 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance.
