# Gizmo

Gizmo, real name Mikron O'Jeneus, is a bad-aligned male character created by DC Comics, first appearing in New Teen Titans #3. He works as an Inventor · Criminal · Businessman and is affiliated with The Society and H.I.V.E. He has Green eyes and Brown hair and stands 127 cm tall and weighs 39 kg. His notable abilities include Flight, Intelligence, Jump, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master.
