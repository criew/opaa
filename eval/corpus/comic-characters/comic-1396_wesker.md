# Wesker

Wesker, real name Albert Wesker, is a bad-aligned male Human / Altered character created by Capcom, first appearing in Resident Evil (1996). He works as an Umbrella researcher/security officer (1978-1998); United States Army commissioned engineering officer (1991-1996); S.T.A.R.S. Captain (1996-1998); H.C.F. operative (1998); Rival company employee (1998);. He has Orange eyes and Blond hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 21.
