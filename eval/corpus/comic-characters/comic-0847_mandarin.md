# Mandarin

Mandarin, real name Khan, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in China and first appearing in Tales of Suspense #50 (February, 1964). He works as a Would-be conqueror; former janitor, businessman and crimelord, government administrator and is affiliated with New Enforcers, National Force and Secret Empire. He has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 97 kg. His notable abilities include Electrokinesis, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Intelligence, Mind Control, Self-Sustenance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
