# Ragman (CW)

Ragman (CW), real name Rory Regan, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Havenrock, Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 5: Episode 2. He works as a Vigilante, Mechanic. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Magic, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 90 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
