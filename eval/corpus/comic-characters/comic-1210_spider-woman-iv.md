# Spider-Woman IV

Spider-Woman IV, real name Charlotte Witter, is a bad-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man Volume 2 #5 (# 446). She works as a Fashion designer, professional criminal. She has Red eyes and White hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 58 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Energy Blasts, Flight, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated across attributes, she scores 70 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 60 for speed, 55 for durability and 60 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
