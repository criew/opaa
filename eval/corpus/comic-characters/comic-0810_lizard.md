# Lizard

Lizard, real name Curtis Connors, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Coral Gables, Florida and first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #6. He works as a Research biologist and is affiliated with The FIRM, Secret Empire, Sinister Six and Sinister Twelve. He has Red eyes and No Hair hair and stands 203 cm tall and weighs 230 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Jump, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 60 for speed, 70 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
