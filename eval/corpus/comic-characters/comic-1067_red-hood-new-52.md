# Red Hood (New 52)

Red Hood (New 52), real name Jason Todd, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham city and first appearing in Red Hood and the Outlaws #1 (November, 2011). He works as a Vigilante, Mercenary, Anti-hero. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 45 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
