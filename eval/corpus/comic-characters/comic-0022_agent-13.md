# Agent 13

Agent 13, real name Sharon Carter, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Tales of Suspense #75. She works as an Agent of SHIELD and is affiliated with Marvel Knights, Fantastic Four, S.H.I.E.L.D., SHIELD and Avengers. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 70 for speed, 70 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
