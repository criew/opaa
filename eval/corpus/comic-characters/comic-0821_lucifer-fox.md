# Lucifer (FOX)

Lucifer (FOX), real name Samael, is a neutral-aligned male Demon character created by DC Comics, born in Heaven. He works as a Nightclub owner, LAPD Civilian consultant, Ruler of Hell (formerly). He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 75 for speed, 100 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 43.
