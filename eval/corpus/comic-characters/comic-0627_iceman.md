# Iceman

Iceman, real name Bobby Drake, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Fort Washington, Long Island, New York and first appearing in X-Men #1. He works as an Adventurer, formerly teacher, accountant, student and is affiliated with X-Men, Ultimate X-Men, Cerebro's X-Men, Secret Defenders, Apocalypse's Horsemen, Champions, Champions of Los Angeles and X-Factor. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 65 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Energy Blasts, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Size Changing, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 55 for speed, 100 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
