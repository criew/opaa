# Venom

Venom, real name Eddie Brock, is a bad-aligned male Symbiote character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York, New York and first appearing in (as alien costume) Secret Wars #8, (behind the scenes) Web of Spider-Man #18, (actual appearance) Amazing Spider-Man #300. He works as a Vigilante; former journalist for the Daily Globe, government operative and is affiliated with Sinister Six. He has Blue eyes and Strawberry Blond hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 117 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Jump, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 65 for speed, 85 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
