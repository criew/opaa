# Aurora

Aurora, real name Jeanne-Marie Beaubier, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Montreal, Quebec, Canada and first appearing in X-Men #121 (1979). She works as an Adventurer; former terrorist, nun, history/geography teacher and is affiliated with Neo-Knights, Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Alpha Flight, X-Men, Weapon X, Headbangers and Children of the Vault. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Energy Blasts, Flight, Reflexes and Super Speed. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 95 for speed, 60 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
