# Collector

Collector, real name Taneleer Tivan, is a bad-aligned male Elder character created by Marvel Comics, born in Cygnus X-1 and first appearing in Avengers #28. He works as a Collector; Cosmic Being and is affiliated with Elders of the Universe. He has White eyes and White hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 203 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Immortality, Invulnerability, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Regeneration and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 30 for speed, 60 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
