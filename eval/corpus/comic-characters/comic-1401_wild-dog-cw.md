# Wild Dog (CW)

Wild Dog (CW), real name Rene Ramirez, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Starling City and first appearing in Arrow Season 5: Episode 1. He works as a Vigilante; formerly Navy Seal. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 71 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 45 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
