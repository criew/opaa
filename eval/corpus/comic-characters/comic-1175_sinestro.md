# Sinestro

Sinestro, real name Thaal Sinestro, is a neutral-aligned male Korugaran character created by DC Comics, born in Korugar City, Korugar and first appearing in Green Lantern Vol 2 #7. He is affiliated with Sinestro Corps, The Society, Green Lantern Corps and Suicide Squad. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 201 cm tall and weighs 92 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Flight, Force Fields, Marksmanship, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 55 for speed, 65 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
