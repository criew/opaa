# Snowbird

Snowbird, real name Narya, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, born in Near Resolute Bay, Northwest Territories, Canada and first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #120. She works as a Goddess, adventurer, former records officer and is affiliated with Alpha Flight. She has White eyes and Blond hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 49 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Shapeshifting, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 50 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
