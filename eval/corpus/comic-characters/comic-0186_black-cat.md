# Black Cat

Black Cat, real name Felicia Hardy, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Queens, New York and first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #194 (July, 1979). She works as a Cat burglar; Private investigator, founder of Cat's Eye Investigations and is affiliated with Black Cat's Gang, Witches, The FIRM, Heroes For Hire and Midnight Sons. She has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 10 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 6.
