# Sarab (CW)

Sarab (CW), real name Maseo Yamashiro, is a neutral-aligned male Human character, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 3: Episode 1. He works as an Assassin; formerly Agent Handler. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 45 for speed, 45 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
