# Ultragirl

Ultragirl, real name Tsu-Zana, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics. She works as a Model and is affiliated with Avengers Resistance. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 105 kg.
