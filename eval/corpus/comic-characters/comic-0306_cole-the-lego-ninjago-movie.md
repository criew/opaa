# Cole (The LEGO Ninjago Movie)

Cole (The LEGO Ninjago Movie), real name Cole, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Lego, born in Ninjago city and first appearing in Ninjago movie. He works as a Ninja. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 58 kg.
