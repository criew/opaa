# Goblin Queen

Goblin Queen, real name Madelyne Jennifer Pryor, is a bad-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, born in Created in Sinister's Labs, Nebraska and first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #168. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Telekinesis and Telepathy.
