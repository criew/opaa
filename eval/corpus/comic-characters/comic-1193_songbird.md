# Songbird

Songbird, real name Melissa Joan Gold, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, born in Shoshoni, Wyoming. She works as an Adventurer; former criminal and wrestler and is affiliated with A.I.M. and Thunderbolts. She has Green eyes and Red / White hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 65 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Flight, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
