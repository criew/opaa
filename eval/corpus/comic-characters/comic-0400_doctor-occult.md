# Doctor Occult

Doctor Occult, real name Unknown, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in American Mid West and first appearing in (Occult): New Fun Comics #6 (October 1935). He works as a powerful magus, Occult Investigator, Warrior and is affiliated with Sentinels of Magic. He has Grey eyes and Black hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 85 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Element Control, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Intelligence, Longevity, Magic, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 100 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 61.
