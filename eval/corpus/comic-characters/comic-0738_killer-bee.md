# Killer Bee

Killer Bee is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Kumogakure and first appearing in Naruto Chapter #408. He works as a Guardian of Kumogakure, Jinchūriki. He has White hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 86 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Electrokinesis, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 70 for speed, 95 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 14.
