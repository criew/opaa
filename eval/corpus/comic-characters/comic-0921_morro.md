# Morro

Morro, real name Morro, is a neutral-aligned character created by Lego, born in Ninjago and first appearing in The Corridor of Elders/70736 Attack of the Morro Dragon. They work as a Second-in-Command of the Ghost Warriors. They have Black eyes and Black hair. Their notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Shapeshifting and Stamina. Rated across attributes, they score 100 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 80 for speed, 100 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 21.
