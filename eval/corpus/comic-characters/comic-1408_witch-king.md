# Witch King

Witch King, real name Witch-King of Angmar, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by J. R. R. Tolkien, born in Middle-earth and first appearing in Lord of the Rings #1. He works as a King of Angmar, Lord of Minas Morgul, Lord of the Nazgûl, Ruler of Númenor, Right hand servant of Sauron. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Fire Control, Immortality, Invulnerability, Longevity, Magic, Stamina and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 65 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 65 for speed, 90 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 12.
