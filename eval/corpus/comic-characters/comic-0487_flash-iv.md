# Flash IV

Flash IV, real name Bartholomew Allen II, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in (as Impulse) Flash v.2 #91 (June 1994)* (as Kid Flash) Teen Titans v.3 #4 (December 2003). He works as a Police trainee and is affiliated with Flash Family. He has Yellow eyes and Auburn hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Dimensional Travel, Energy Constructs, Reflexes and Super Speed. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 100 for speed, 30 for durability and 50 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
