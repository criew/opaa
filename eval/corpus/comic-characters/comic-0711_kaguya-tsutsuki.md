# Kaguya Ōtsutsuki

Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, real name Ōtsutsuki Kaguya, is a bad-aligned female God / Eternal character created by Shueisha, first appearing in Naruto, Volume #70, Chapter #679. She works as a Princess. She has White eyes and White hair. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Element Control, Flight, Illusions, Immortality, Invulnerability, Mind Control, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, she scores 55 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 37.
