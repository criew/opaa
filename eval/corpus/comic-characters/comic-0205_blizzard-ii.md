# Blizzard II

Blizzard II, real name Donald Gill, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Newark, Delaware and first appearing in Iron Man #223 (1987). He works as an Adventurer; former mercenary and professional criminal and is affiliated with National Force. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. Rated across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 4.
