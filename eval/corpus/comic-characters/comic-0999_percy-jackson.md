# Percy Jackson

Percy Jackson, real name Percy Jackson, is a good-aligned male Demi-God character, born in New York City and first appearing in The Lightning Theif. He works as a Hero and is affiliated with New Justice Team and Joker League of Anarchy. He has Green / Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Magic, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 80 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 20.
