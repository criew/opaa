# Cable (FOX)

Cable (FOX), real name Nathan Summers, is a bad-aligned male Cyborg character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Deadpool 2. He has Red eyes and Brown hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Element Control, Force Fields, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 65 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 80 for speed, 60 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
