# Spawn

Spawn, real name Al Simmons, is a good-aligned male Demon character created by Image Comics, born in Detroit, Michigan (8th level of Hell after rebirth) and first appearing in Spawn #1 (May, 1992). He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 211 cm tall and weighs 405 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Flight, Immortality, Invulnerability, Magic, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy, Teleportation and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 70 for strength, 50 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 19.
