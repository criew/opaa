# Spike

Spike, real name William Pratt, is a neutral-aligned male Vampire character created by Dark Horse Comics, first appearing in Buffy the Vampire Slayer: Spike and Dru #1. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 73 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 50 for speed, 65 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 22.
