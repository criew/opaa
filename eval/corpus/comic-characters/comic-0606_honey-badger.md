# Honey Badger

Honey Badger, real name Gabrielle Kinney, is a good-aligned female Clone character created by Marvel Comics, born in Alchemax Genetics, North America and first appearing in All-New Wolverine #2. She works as a Vigilante, adventurer. She has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 137 cm tall. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 50 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 75 for speed, 65 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 3.
