# Sabretooth

Sabretooth, real name Victor Creed, is a bad-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Iron Fist #14 (August, 1977). He works as a mercenary, professional criminal, assassin, serial killer and is affiliated with Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, Weapon X, Brotherhood of Mutants and Marauders. He has Amber eyes and Blond hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 171 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 40 for speed, 90 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
