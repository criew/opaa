# Kisame

Kisame, real name Kisame Hoshigaki, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, first appearing in Volume #16, Naruto Chapter #139. He works as a Member of the Akatsuki, Member of the Seven, Swordsmen of the Mist. He has White eyes and Grey hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 82 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 75 for speed, 95 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 16.
