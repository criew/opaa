# Jesse Quick

Jesse Quick, real name Jesse Chambers, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Queens New York City (New Earth) and first appearing in Justice Society of America Vol 2 #1. She works as an Adventurer, Businesswoman, Speedsters and is affiliated with Flash Family, Justice League, Justice Legion Alpha and Justice Society of America. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Intelligence, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 100 for speed, 60 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 13.
