# Nekron

Nekron, real name Nekron, is a bad-aligned male God / Eternal character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Justice League of America #128 - "Death-Visions of the Justice League!". He works as a Lord of The Unliving and is affiliated with Black Lantern Corps. He has Black eyes and is bald. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Immortality, Invulnerability, Longevity, Magic, Reality Warping, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 75 for speed, 95 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 80.
