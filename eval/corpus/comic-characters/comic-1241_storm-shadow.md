# Storm Shadow

Storm Shadow, real name Tomisaburo Arashikage, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by IDW Publishing, born in St. Louis, Missouri and first appearing in G.I. Joe: A Real American Hero #21 - Silent Interlude. He works as a Mercenary, Ninja. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
