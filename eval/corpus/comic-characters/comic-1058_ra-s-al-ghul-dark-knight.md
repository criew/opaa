# Ra's Al Ghul (Dark Knight)

Ra's Al Ghul (Dark Knight), real name Ra's Al Ghul, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Batman begins (June, 2005). He works as a Leader of the league of Assassins, Mercenary. He has Blue eyes and Brown / White hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 97 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 30 for speed, 40 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
