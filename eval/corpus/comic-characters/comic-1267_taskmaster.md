# Taskmaster

Taskmaster, real name Tony Masters, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Avengers #195. He works as a Sheriff, Assassin, Mercenary; formerly Military Trainer, Combat Instructor, Head of Initiative training camp and is affiliated with The FIRM, Agency X, New Enforcers, Superiour Six, Sinister Seven and Secret Empire. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 50 for speed, 20 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
