# Silver Samurai (FOX)

Silver Samurai (FOX), real name Ichirō Yashida, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Japan and first appearing in The Wolverine (2013). He works as a Chairman of Yashida Zaibatsu. He has Brown eyes and Grey hair and stands 234 cm tall and weighs 162 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 30 for speed, 80 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
