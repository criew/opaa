# Madame Xanadu

Madame Xanadu, real name Nimue Inwudu, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in England and first appearing in Doorway to Nightmare #1 - Doorway to Nightmare. She works as a Magician,Occultist and is affiliated with Sentinels of Magic. She has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 56 kg. Her notable abilities include Electrokinesis, Energy Constructs, Force Fields, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Magic, Stamina, Stealth, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 50 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 18.
