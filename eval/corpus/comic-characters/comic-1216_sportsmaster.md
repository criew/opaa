# Sportsmaster

Sportsmaster, real name Lawrence Crock, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in All-American Comics #85 - The Rise and Fall of 'Crusher' Crock. He works as a Professional Criminal and is affiliated with Extreme Justice. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Reflexes and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 40 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
