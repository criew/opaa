# Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad

Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad, real name Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Ubisoft, born in Masyaf, Syria and first appearing in Assassin's Creed #1 - Assassin's Creed. He works as a Mentor of Assassin Order, Assassin. He has Hazel eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Manipulation, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 40 for speed, 40 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
