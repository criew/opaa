# Barb Wire

Barb Wire, real name Barbara Kopetski, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Dark Horse Comics, first appearing in Comics' Greatest World: Steel Harbor #2 - The Machine. She works as a Bar Owner; Bounty Hunter. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 25 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
