# Connor Kenway

Connor Kenway, real name Ratonhnhaké:ton, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Ubisoft, born in Mohawk Valley, British America and first appearing in Assassin's Creed III. He works as a Master Assassin, Assassin. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 77 kg.
