# Crystal (MCU)

Crystal (MCU), real name Crystalia Amaquelin, is a good-aligned female Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in Attilan and first appearing in Inhumans Season 1: Episode 1. She works as a Princess of Attilan and is affiliated with A-Force. She has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Electrokinesis, Element Control, Fire Control and Stamina. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 20 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
