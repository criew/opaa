# Green Arrow

Green Arrow, real name Oliver Queen, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in More Fun Comics #73 (November 1941). He works as a Professional Crime-fighter; Multi-Billionaire and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha, All-Star Squadron, Black Lantern Corps and Justice League. He has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 88 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Endurance, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 25 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
