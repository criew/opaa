# Doctor Mid-Nite

Doctor Mid-Nite, real name Dr Charles Mcnider, is a good-aligned male Human / Altered character created by DC Comics, born in An unidentified American city and first appearing in All-American Comics #25 (April 1941). He works as a Doctor, Surgeon, Superhero, Author and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha and Justice Society of America. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stealth and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 35 for speed, 35 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
