# Sensei Garmadon

Sensei Garmadon, real name Garmadon, is a good-aligned male Human character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Season 2 episode 13. He works as a Sensei. He has Black eyes and Grey hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
