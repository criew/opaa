# Cosmic King Thor

Cosmic King Thor, real name Thor Odinson, is a good-aligned male God / Eternal character created by Marvel Comics, born in Asgard and first appearing in Journey Into Mystery (1962), #83 (Thor's first appearance) | Thor (2020), #1 (Thor's first appearance as Herald of Thunder). He works as an Adventurer. He has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 288 kg.
