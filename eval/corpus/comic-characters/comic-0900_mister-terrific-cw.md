# Mister Terrific (CW)

Mister Terrific (CW), real name Curtis Holt, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Starling City, Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 4: Episode 2. He works as a Vigilante; formerly Olympic Athlete. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 40 for speed, 35 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
