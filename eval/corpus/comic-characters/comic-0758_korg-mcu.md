# Korg (MCU)

Korg (MCU), real name Korg, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Ria and first appearing in Thor Ragnarok. He has Blue eyes and Blue hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 75 for strength, 60 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
