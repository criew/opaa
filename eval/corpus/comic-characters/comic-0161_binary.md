# Binary

Binary, real name Carol Danvers, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, born in Boston, Massachusetts and first appearing in (as Carol Danvers) MARVEL SUPER HEROES #13, (as Ms. Marvel) MS. MARVEL #1, (as Binary) X-MEN # 164, AVENGERS Vol. 3, # 4. She works as a Former National Aeronautics and Space Administration security Chief, former magazine editor, former freelance writer, former military intelligence officer. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Dexterity, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Invulnerability, Matter Manipulation, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 95 for speed, 100 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 20.
