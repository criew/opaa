# Steppenwolf

Steppenwolf is a bad-aligned male New God character created by DC Comics, first appearing in The New Gods #7. He is affiliated with New Gods of Apokolips. He has Red eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 91 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Senses, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 85 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 19.
