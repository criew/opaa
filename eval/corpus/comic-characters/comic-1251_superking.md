# Superking

Superking, real name Bender, is a neutral-aligned male Robot character created by Matt Groening, born in Earth, Mexico, Tijuana and first appearing in Space Pilot 3000. He works as an Industrial Robot, Girder-bender, Wrestler, Soldier, Planet Express Worker. He has Black eyes and is bald and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 270 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Electrokinesis, Enhanced Hearing, Invulnerability, Longevity, Stealth and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 40 for speed, 90 for durability and 60 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
