# Beta Ray Bill

Beta Ray Bill, real name Beta Ray Bill, is a good-aligned male Leprechaun character created by Marvel Comics, born in Burning Galaxy and first appearing in Thor #337. He works as a Guardian of the Korbinite race and is affiliated with Thor Corps, Gods, Annihilators and God Squad. He has No Hair hair and stands 201 cm tall and weighs 216 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Immortality, Invulnerability, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 35 for speed, 95 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
