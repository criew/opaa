# Karnak

Karnak, real name Karnak Mander-Azur, is a male Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in Attilan City, North Atlantic and first appearing in Fantastic Four #45. He works as a Magister of the Second Tower of Wisdom, priest/philosopher, adventurer and is affiliated with Inhumans. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Endurance, Reflexes, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 65 for speed, 50 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
