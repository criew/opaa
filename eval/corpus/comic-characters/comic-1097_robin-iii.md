# Robin III

Robin III, real name Tim Drake, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Batman #436 (August, 1989). He works as a High School Student and is affiliated with Teen Titans, Batman Family and Young Justice. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 56 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
