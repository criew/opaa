# Nagato Uzumaki

Nagato Uzumaki, real name Nagato Uzumaki, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Amegakure(The Village Hidden in the Rain) and first appearing in Volume #41, Naruto Chapter #372. He works as an Akatsuki Leader,. He has Violet eyes and Red / Orange hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 40 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Element Control, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Teleportation, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 50 for speed, 80 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 29.
