# Soranik Natu

Soranik Natu, real name Soranik Sinestro, is a neutral-aligned female Alien character created by DC Comics, born in Korugar and first appearing in Green Lantern Corps: Recharge #1 - The Gathering. She works as a Neurosurgeon. She has Purple eyes and Black hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 78 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 50 for speed, 55 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 12.
