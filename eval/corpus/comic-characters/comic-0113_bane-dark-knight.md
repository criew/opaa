# Bane (Dark Knight)

Bane (Dark Knight), real name Unknown, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Santa prisca and first appearing in The Dark Knight Rises (July, 2012). He works as a Mercenary. He has Hazel eyes and No Hair hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 104 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 25 for speed, 60 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
