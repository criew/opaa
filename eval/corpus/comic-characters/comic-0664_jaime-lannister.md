# Jaime Lannister

Jaime Lannister is a good-aligned male Human character created by George R. R. Martin, born in Castlerly Rock and first appearing in A Song of Ice & Fire; Book 1: A Game Of Thrones.. He works as a Knight, Lord Commander of the Kingsguard. He has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 86 kg. His notable abilities include Dexterity, Reflexes, Stamina and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 60 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 45 for speed, 35 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 2.
