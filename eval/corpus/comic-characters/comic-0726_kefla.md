# Kefla

Kefla, real name Kale/Caulifla, is a neutral-aligned female Saiyan character created by Shueisha, born in Universe 6 and first appearing in Dragon Ball Super: Chapter 38 (July 21, 2018). She works as a Potara Fusion. She has Black eyes and Black hair. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Illusions, Invulnerability, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 17.
