# Black Spider

Black Spider, real name Eric Needham, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Detective Comics #463. He works as an Assassin, Vigilante and is affiliated with Suicide Squad and League of Assassins. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 3 cm tall and weighs 171 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Marksmanship and Reflexes. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 35 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
