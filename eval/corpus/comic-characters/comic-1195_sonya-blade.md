# Sonya Blade

Sonya Blade is a good-aligned female Human character created by Mortal Kombat, born in Austin, Texas and first appearing in Mortal Kombat. She works as an U.S. Special Forces Lieutenant. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 65 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
