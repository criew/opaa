# Man-Thing

Man-Thing, real name Dr. Theodore Sallis, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Savage Tales #1 (May, 1971). He is affiliated with Midnight Sons, A.R.M.O.R. and Thunderbolts. He has Red eyes and No Hair hair and stands 213 cm tall and weighs 225 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Immortality, Invulnerability, Stealth, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 10 for speed, 100 for durability and 40 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
