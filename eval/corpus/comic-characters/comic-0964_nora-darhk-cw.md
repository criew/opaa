# Nora Darhk (CW)

Nora Darhk (CW), real name Eleanor Darhk, is a neutral-aligned female Human character, born in Starling City, Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 4: Episode 9. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Electrokinesis, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Magic, Telekinesis and Teleportation.
