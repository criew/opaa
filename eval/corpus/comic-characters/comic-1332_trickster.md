# Trickster

Trickster, real name Giovanni Giuseppe, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Flash #113 (July, 1960). He works as a Special effects designer, con artist,Professional Criminal, and is affiliated with Living Assault Weapons, Minutemen and Rogues. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Flight, Intelligence, Reflexes, Stealth and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 65 for speed, 25 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
