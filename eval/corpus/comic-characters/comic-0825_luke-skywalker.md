# Luke Skywalker

Luke Skywalker, real name Luke Skywalker, is a good-aligned male Human character created by George Lucas, born in Tatooine and first appearing in Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope (1977). He is affiliated with Jedi Order and Jedi Council. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stealth, Super Speed, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 40 for speed, 25 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
