# Valkyrie (MCU)

Valkyrie (MCU), real name Brunnhilde, is a good-aligned female Asgardian character created by Marvel Comics, born in Asgard and first appearing in Thor Ragnarok. She works as a Warrior. She has Black eyes and Brown / Black hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Longevity, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes and Regeneration. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 85 for speed, 95 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 14.
