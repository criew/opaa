# Jacob Frye

Jacob Frye, real name Jacob Frye, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Ubisoft, born in London and first appearing in Assassin's Creed Syndicate. He works as an Assassin. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 72 kg.
