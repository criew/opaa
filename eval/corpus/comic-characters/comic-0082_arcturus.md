# Arcturus

Arcturus, real name Arcturus, is a neutral-aligned male Animal character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Spellbound (Flashback). He works as an Anacondrai General. He has Green eyes and is bald and stands 4000 cm tall and weighs 119 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 55 for speed, 80 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 11.
