# Nightwing (New 52)

Nightwing (New 52), real name Dick Grayson, is a male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Nightwing Vol 3 #1. He works as a Cab Driver · formerly Bartender, Casino Dealer, Personal Trainer, Secret Agent, Teacher, Vigilante. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 78 kg.
