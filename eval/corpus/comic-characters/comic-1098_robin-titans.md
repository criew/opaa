# Robin (Titans)

Robin (Titans), real name Jason Todd, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Titans Season 1: Episode 5. He works as a Vigilante. He has Green eyes and Brown hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master.
