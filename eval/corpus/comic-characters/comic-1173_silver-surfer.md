# Silver Surfer

Silver Surfer, real name Norrin Radd, is a good-aligned male Alien character created by Marvel Comics, born in Zenn-La and first appearing in Fantastic Four, Vol. 1 #48. He works as an Adventurer, formerly Galactus' Herald and is affiliated with Defenders, Neo-Knights, Contingency, Secret Defenders, Annihilators, God Squad, Avengers, Star Masters and Heralds of Galactus. He has White eyes and is bald and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Immortality, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 90 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 47.
