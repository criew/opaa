# Captain Boomerang

Captain Boomerang, real name George Harkness, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Australia and first appearing in The Flash #117 - Here Comes Captain Boomerang! / The Mad-cap Inventors of Central City!. He works as a Professional Criminal and is affiliated with Suicide Squad, Rogues, Living Assault Weapons and Super Buddies. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 75 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 60 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 40 for speed, 40 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 2.
