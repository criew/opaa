# Sonic The Hedgehog

Sonic The Hedgehog, real name Sonic, is a good-aligned male Animal character created by Sega, born in Christmas Island and first appearing in Sonic the Hedgehog (1991). He has Green eyes and Blue hair and stands 99 cm tall and weighs 35 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 100 for speed, 75 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 53.
