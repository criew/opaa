# Vixen II (CW)

Vixen II (CW), real name Mari, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Zambesi, Earth-1 and first appearing in Vixen Season 1: Episode 1. She works as an Adventurer, Vigilante, Fashion Designer. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Jump, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 70 for speed, 60 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 8.
