# Edward Kenway

Edward Kenway, real name Edward Kenway, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Ubisoft, born in Swansea, Wales and first appearing in Assassin's Creed IV Black Flag. He works as a Pirate, Master Assassin. He has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Manipulation, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 40 for speed, 40 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
