# Skales

Skales, real name Skales, is a neutral-aligned male Animal character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Rise of the Snakes /9444 Cole's Tread Assault. He works as a General of the Hypnobrai. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 599 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 65 for speed, 80 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 20.
