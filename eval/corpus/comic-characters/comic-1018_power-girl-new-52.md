# Power Girl (New 52)

Power Girl (New 52), real name Kara Zor-L, is a good-aligned female Kryptonian character created by DC Comics, born in Kandor, Krypton and first appearing in All-Star Comics #58. She works as a Businesswoman. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Energy Absorption, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Invulnerability, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 90 for speed, 100 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 15.
