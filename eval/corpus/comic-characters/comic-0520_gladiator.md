# Gladiator

Gladiator, real name Kallark, is a neutral-aligned male Strontian character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in X-Men Vol 1 #107 October, 1977. He works as a Majestor of the Shi'ar Empire; Former Praetor of the Imperial Guard; possibly former Herald of Galactus and is affiliated with Imperial Guard, Gods and Annihilators. He has Blue eyes and Blue hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 268 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Invulnerability, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
