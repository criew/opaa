# Master Chief

Master Chief, real name John-117, is a good-aligned male Human / Altered character created by Microsoft, born in Elysium City, Eridanus II. He works as a Master Chief Petty Officer of the Navy. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 213 cm tall. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Longevity, Marksmanship, Stamina, Super Speed and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 75 for strength, 35 for speed, 80 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
