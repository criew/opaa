# Red Hulk (Ghost Rider) (Venom)

Red Hulk (Ghost Rider) (Venom), real name Thaddeus Ross, is a neutral-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Venom Vol 2 #13 (April, 2012). He works as a Lieutenant general in US Air Force. He is bald and stands 213 cm tall and weighs 540 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Matter Manipulation, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 35.
