# Captain Soto

Captain Soto, real name Soto, is a neutral-aligned male Human character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Season 2, Episode 2 Pirates Vs. Ninja / Set 70591 Kryptarium Prison Breakout. He works as a Pirate. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 1500 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 50 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
