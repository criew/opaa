# Etrigan

Etrigan, real name Jason Blood, is a neutral-aligned male Demon character created by DC Comics, born in Hell and first appearing in Demon #1 (September, 1972). He is affiliated with Demon Knights. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 203 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Senses, Immortality, Jump, Magic and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 15 for speed, 100 for durability and 50 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
