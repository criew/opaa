# Ultimo

Ultimo, real name Doomsday Device, is a bad-aligned male Android character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Tales of Suspense #76 (April, 1966). He has Yellow eyes and is bald and stands 3050 cm tall. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Invulnerability, Matter Manipulation, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 11.
