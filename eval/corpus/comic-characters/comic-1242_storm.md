# Storm

Storm, real name Ororo Munroe, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York, New York and first appearing in Giant-Size X-Men #1 (May, 1975). She works as an Adventurer and is affiliated with X-Men, Morlocks, Neo-Knights, Ultimate X-Men, Cerebro's X-Men and Fantastic Four. She has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 57 kg. Her notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence and Telepathy Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 45 for speed, 30 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 8.
