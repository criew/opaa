# Kakashi Hatake

Kakashi Hatake, real name Kakashi Hatake, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, first appearing in Volume #1, Naruto Chapter #3. He works as an Anbu Captain, Third Division Commander of the Allied Shinobi Forces, Hokage and is affiliated with Team 7. He has Black eyes and Grey hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 67 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Electrokinesis, Energy Blasts, Fire Control, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 85 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
