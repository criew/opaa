# Starling

Starling, real name Evelyn Crawford, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Birds of Prey #1 (September, 2011). She is affiliated with Birds of Prey. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 69 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility and Weapons Master.
