# Homelander

Homelander, real name John, is a bad-aligned male Alien character, first appearing in The Boys #3 - Cherry, Part One (November 2006). He works as a Leader of The Seven. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 74 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 20.
