# Sunspot

Sunspot, real name Roberto DaCosta, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil and first appearing in Marvel Graphic Novel #4 (October, 1982). He works as an Adventurer, businessman and is affiliated with Young X-Men, X-Force, X-Men and New Mutants. He has brown eyes and black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Energy Blasts, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 35 for speed, 25 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
