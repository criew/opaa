# Cosmo The Spacedog

Cosmo The Spacedog, real name Cosmo, is a good-aligned male Animal character created by Marvel Comics, born in Soviet Russia and first appearing in Nova vol. 4 #8. He works as an Astronaut, liason for the new Guardians of the Galaxy, Chief Of Security On Knowhere. He has Brown eyes and Yellow hair and stands 58 cm tall and weighs 32 kg. His notable abilities include Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 45 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
