# Huntress (CW)

Huntress (CW), real name Helena Bertinelli, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Arrow season 1. She works as a Vigilante (formerly). She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 45 for speed, 30 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
