# Doc Samson

Doc Samson, real name Leonard Samson, is a good-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, born in Tulsa, Oklahoma and first appearing in Incredible Hulk #141 (July, 1971). He works as a Psychiatrist, teacher, adventurer, former special consultant to Gamma Base. He has Blue eyes and Green hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 171 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 35 for speed, 80 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
