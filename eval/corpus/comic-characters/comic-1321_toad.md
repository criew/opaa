# Toad

Toad, real name Mortimer Toynbee, is a neutral-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in York, England and first appearing in X-Men #4 (March, 1964). He works as a Former criminal and is affiliated with Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, Brotherhood of Mutants and Nasty Boys. He has Black eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 76 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Jump, Mind Control, Reflexes, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 60 for speed, 55 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
