# Warpath

Warpath, real name James Proudstar, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Camp Verde, Arizona and first appearing in New Mutants #16 (June, 1984). He works as an Adventurer and is affiliated with X-Force. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 218 cm tall and weighs 158 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Heat Resistance, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 70 for strength, 45 for speed, 70 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
