# Nebula (MCU)

Nebula (MCU), real name Nebula, is a neutral-aligned female Titanian character created by Marvel Comics, born in Titan and first appearing in Guardians of the Galaxy Prelude. She works as a Galaxy class killer. She has Blue eyes and No Hair hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Peak Human Condition, Regeneration, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 50 for speed, 55 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
