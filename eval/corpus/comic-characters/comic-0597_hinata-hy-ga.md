# Hinata Hyūga

Hinata Hyūga, real name Hinata Hyūga, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Volume #4, Naruto Chapter #34. She works as a Heiress of the Hyūga Clan. She has White eyes and Black hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Force Fields, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 70 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
