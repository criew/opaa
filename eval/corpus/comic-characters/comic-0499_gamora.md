# Gamora

Gamora, real name Gamora Zen Whoberi Ben Titan, is a good-aligned female Zen-Whoberian character created by Marvel Comics, born in Planet Zen Whoberi, Silican System, Milky Way and first appearing in Strange Tales #180 (June, 1975). She works as an Assassin, mercenary, adventurer and is affiliated with Guardians of the Galaxy, Marvel Knights and The Mighty Avengers. She has Yellow eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 40 for speed, 85 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 9.
