# Doctor Octopus (Shattered Dimensions)

Doctor Octopus (Shattered Dimensions), real name Serena Patel, is a female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Transverse City and first appearing in Spider-Man: Shattered Dimensions. She works as a Director of Alchemax's Shadow Division. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Intelligence, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 50 for speed, 40 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
