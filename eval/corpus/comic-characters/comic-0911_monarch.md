# Monarch

Monarch, real name Nathaniel Christopher Adam, is a good-aligned male character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Captain Atom #1 (D.C., March, 1987). He works as an Adventurer, former U.S. Special Agent, former U.S. Air Force Captain. He has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Flight, Force Fields, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Self-Sustenance, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telekinesis. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 95 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 54.
