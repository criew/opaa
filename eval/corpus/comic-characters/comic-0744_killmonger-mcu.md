# Killmonger (MCU)

Killmonger (MCU), real name Erik Stevens, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Oakland, California and first appearing in Black Panther (2018). He works as a former King of Wakanda. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 60 for speed, 80 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
