# T-800

T-800, real name Cyberdyne Systems Series 800 Terminator Model 101, is a bad-aligned male Cyborg character created by Dark Horse Comics, first appearing in The Terminator (1984). He works as an Assassin and is affiliated with Skynet. He has Red eyes and weighs 176 kg. His notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Longevity, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 15 for speed, 60 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
