# Doppelganger

Doppelganger is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in The Dimension of Manifestations and first appearing in Infinity War #1 (1992). He is affiliated with Carnage Family. He has White eyes and No Hair hair and stands 196 cm tall and weighs 104 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 45 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 60 for speed, 95 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 4.
