# Desmond Miles

Desmond Miles, real name Desmond Miles, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Ubisoft, born in Black Hills, South Dakota, United States and first appearing in Assassin's Creed. He works as an Assassin. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Manipulation, Intelligence, Mind Control, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 30 for speed, 40 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
