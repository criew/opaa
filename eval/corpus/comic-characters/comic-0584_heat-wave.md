# Heat Wave

Heat Wave, real name Mick Rory, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Flash #140 (November, 1963). He works as a Professional Criminal and is affiliated with Rogues, Justice Legion Alpha, Living Assault Weapons, Minutemen and The Society. He has Blue eyes and is bald and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 15 for speed, 45 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
