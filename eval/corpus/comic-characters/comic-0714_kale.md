# Kale

Kale, real name Kale, is a neutral-aligned female Saiyan character created by Shueisha, born in Universe 6 and first appearing in Dragon Ball Super: Volume 6, Chapter 32 (January 20, 2018). She works as a Member of the Saiyan Punks. She has Black eyes and Black hair. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Illusions, Invulnerability, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 17.
