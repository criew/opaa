# Red Skull

Red Skull, real name Johann Shmidt, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in An unidentified village in Germany and first appearing in Captain America Comics #1 (1941), (modern) Tales of Suspense #66 (1965). He is affiliated with HYDRA and Masters of Evil. He has Blue eyes and is bald and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 108 kg. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 10 for speed, 15 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
