# Vision

Vision, real name Vision, is a good-aligned male Android character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Avengers Vol.1 #57, Young Avengers # 4. He is affiliated with Contingency, Avengers, Avengers West Coast, The Mighty Avengers, Mighty Avengers, Marvel Knights, West Coast Avengers, Queen's Vengeance, Defenders and Agency X. He has Gold eyes and is bald and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 135 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 70 for strength, 55 for speed, 95 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
