# Preeminent

Preeminent, real name The Preeminent, is a bad-aligned female God / Eternal character, born in Ethereal Divide and first appearing in Kingdom Come (Illusion). She works as a Cursed realm. She has Green eyes and Green hair and stands 1400 cm tall and weighs 496000 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Cold Resistance, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Invulnerability, Longevity, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 70 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 50 for speed, 100 for durability and 25 for combat, giving an overall score of 70.
