# Blizzard Samurai

Blizzard Samurai, real name Unknown, is a bad-aligned male character created by Lego, born in Neverrealm and first appearing in The Never-Realm. He works as a Warriors. He has Blue / White eyes and No Hair hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 69 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Element Control, Flight, Force Fields, Stamina, Telekinesis, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 45 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
