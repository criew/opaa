# Big Boss

Big Boss, real name John, is a good-aligned male Human character, first appearing in Metal Gear (1987). He works as a Soldier, spy, mercenary, officer, politician. He has Blue eyes and Brown / White hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 88 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
