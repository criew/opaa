# Bane

Bane is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Batman: Vengeance of Bane #1 (January, 1993). He works as a Villian and is affiliated with League of Assassins, Extreme Justice, Living Assault Weapons, Suicide Squad, The Society and Secret Six. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 203 cm tall and weighs 180 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 75 for strength, 50 for speed, 70 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
