# Fandral (MCU)

Fandral (MCU), real name Fandral, is a good-aligned male Asgardian character created by Marvel Comics, born in Asgard and first appearing in Thor (2011). He works as an Adventurer. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 263 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 70 for speed, 70 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
