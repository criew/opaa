# James T. Kirk

James T. Kirk, real name James T. Kirk, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Star Trek, born in Riverside, Iowa, Earth and first appearing in Star Trek S01E01: The Man Trap. He works as a Captain and is affiliated with Federation and Starfleet. He has Hazel eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 15 for speed, 30 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
