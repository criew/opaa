# Citizen Steel

Citizen Steel, real name Nathan Heywood, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Justice Society of America Vol 3 #2 (March, 2007). He works as an Adventurer, former Professional Football Player and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha and Justice League. He has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 170 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Jump, Reflexes, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 35 for speed, 100 for durability and 60 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
