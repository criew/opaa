# Cable

Cable, real name Nathan Summers, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Unrevealed location in the 20th Century United States and first appearing in New Mutants #86 (as Cable). He works as a (current) Adventurer, (former) freedom fighter, United States government agent, mercenary, combat instructor for X-Force and is affiliated with Cerebro's X-Men, Deep Six, X-Men, X-Force and Six Pack. He has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 203 cm tall and weighs 158 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Energy Blasts, Flight, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 25 for speed, 55 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 22.
