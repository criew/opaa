# Emma Frost

Emma Frost, real name Emma Frost, is a neutral-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Boston, Massachusetts and first appearing in X-Men, Vol. 1 #129. She is affiliated with Generation X, Hellfire Club, X-Men and Dark X-Men. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 65 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Electrokinesis, Energy Blasts, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Invulnerability, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Telepathy Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 70 for speed, 100 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 13.
