# Shisui Uchiha

Shisui Uchiha, real name Uchiha Shisui, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Naruto, Volume 49, Chapter 459. He works as a Shinobi, Jonin, Anbu. He has Red eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Electrokinesis, Energy Absorption, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Teleportation and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 85 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 14.
