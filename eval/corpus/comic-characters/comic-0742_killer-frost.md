# Killer Frost

Killer Frost, real name Caitlin Snow, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Fury of Firestorm: The Nuclear Men #19. She works as a Scientist and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha, Minutemen, Justice League of America, Suicide Squad and The Society. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair. Her notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability and Energy Absorption. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 15 for speed, 35 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 8.
