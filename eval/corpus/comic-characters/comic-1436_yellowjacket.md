# Yellowjacket

Yellowjacket, real name Hank Pym, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Elmsford, New York and first appearing in (as Pym) TALES TO ASTONISH #27, (as Ant-Man) TALES TO ASTONISH #35, (as Giant-Man) TALES TO ASTONISH #49, (as Goliath) AVENGERS #28, (as Yellowjacket) AVENGERS #59. He works as an Adventurer, Biochemist, former manager of Avengers Compound and is affiliated with Illuminati. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Size Changing. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 10 for speed, 30 for durability and 15 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
