# Jonah Hex

Jonah Hex is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in All-Star Western #10 - Welcome to Paradise. He works as a Bounty Hunter,soldier and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 85 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
