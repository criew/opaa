# Harley Quinn (New 52)

Harley Quinn (New 52), real name Harleen Quinzel, is a neutral-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Suicide Squad Vol 4 1. She works as a Criminal. She has Blue / White eyes and Red hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Intelligence, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 50 for speed, 65 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
