# Nobu (MCU)

Nobu (MCU), real name Nobu Yoshioka, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Daredevil Season 1: Episode 1. He works as a Crimelord. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 70 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
