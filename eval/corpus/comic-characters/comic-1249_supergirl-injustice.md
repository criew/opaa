# Supergirl (Injustice)

Supergirl (Injustice), real name Kara Zor-El, is a good-aligned female Kryptonian character created by DC Comics, born in Krypton and first appearing in Injustice 2. She works as an Adventurer. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 20.
