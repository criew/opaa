# Arkillo

Arkillo, real name Arkillo, is a bad-aligned male Cosmic Entity character created by DC Comics, born in Vorn and first appearing in Green Lantern Vol 4 #10. He works as a Slave Overseer · Drill Sergeant and is affiliated with Sinestro Corps. He has Black eyes and No Hair hair and stands 208 cm tall and weighs 308 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Beams, Energy Constructs, Flight, Invulnerability, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 65 for speed, 70 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 14.
