# Lady Bullseye

Lady Bullseye, real name Maki Matsumotu, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Japan and first appearing in Daredevil Vol 2 #111 (November, 2008). She works as a Lawyer; Assassin, mercenary. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 45 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
