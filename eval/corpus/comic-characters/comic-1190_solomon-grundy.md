# Solomon Grundy

Solomon Grundy, real name Cyrus Gold, is a bad-aligned male Zombie character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in All-American Comics #61 (October, 1944). He is affiliated with All-Star Squadron, Extreme Justice and Black Lantern Corps. He has Black eyes and White hair and stands 279 cm tall and weighs 437 kg. His notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability, Energy Absorption, Fire Resistance, Invulnerability, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 55 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 15 for speed, 100 for durability and 30 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
