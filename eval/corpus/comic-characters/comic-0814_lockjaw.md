# Lockjaw

Lockjaw, real name Lockjaw, is a good-aligned male Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in Island of Attilan, Atlantic Ocean and first appearing in Fantastic Four #45. He works as a Companion to the Royal Family of the Inhumans and is affiliated with Pet Avengers and Inhumans. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 150 cm tall and weighs 558 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Enhanced Senses, Invulnerability, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 90 for speed, 50 for durability and 30 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
