# Mephisto

Mephisto, real name Mephistopheles, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Hell. He works as a Lord of Mephisto's Hell and is affiliated with Gods, Hell Lords and Demons. He has White eyes and Black hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 140 kg. His notable abilities include Dimensional Travel, Durability, Energy Blasts, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Magic, Matter Manipulation, Reality Warping, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 45 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 64.
