# Kurse

Kurse, real name Algrim, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Svartalfheim and first appearing in Thor #347. He works as a Protector; formerly seeker of vengeance, servant of Malekith. He is bald and stands 213 cm tall and weighs 378 kg. His notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Longevity, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 60 for speed, 95 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
