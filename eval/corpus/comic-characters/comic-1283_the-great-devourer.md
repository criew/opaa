# The Great Devourer

The Great Devourer, real name The Great Devourer, is a bad-aligned female Animal character, born in Unknown and first appearing in Season 1, Episode 5 Can of Worms (pictured) /9450 Epic Dragon Battle. She works as a Serpentine goddess. She has Purple eyes and is bald and stands 998 cm tall and weighs 198 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Element Control, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 60 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 95 for speed, 100 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 32.
