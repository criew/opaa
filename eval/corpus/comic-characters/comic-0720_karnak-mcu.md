# Karnak (MCU)

Karnak (MCU), real name Karnak Mander-Azur, is a good-aligned male Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in Attilan and first appearing in Inhumans Season 1: Episode 1. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 64 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 45 for speed, 55 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
