# Beast Boy (Titans)

Beast Boy (Titans), real name Garfield Logan, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Titans Season 1: Episode 1. He works as an Adventurer. He has Brown eyes and Green hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 60 for speed, 45 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
