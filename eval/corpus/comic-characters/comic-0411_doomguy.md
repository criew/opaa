# Doomguy

Doomguy, real name B.J. Blazkowicz IV, is a good-aligned male Human character, first appearing in Doom (1993). He works as an US Space Marine Captain. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 104 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 65 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 40 for speed, 85 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 24.
