# Littlefinger

Littlefinger, real name Petyr Baelish, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by George R. R. Martin, born in The Fingers and first appearing in A Song of Ice & Fire; Book 1: A Game Of Thrones.. He works as a Lord Protector of the Vale, Master of Coin, Lord of Harrenhall. He has Grey eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. His notable abilities include Intelligence. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 5 for strength, 20 for speed, 15 for durability and 15 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
