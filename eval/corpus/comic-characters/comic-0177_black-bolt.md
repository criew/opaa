# Black Bolt

Black Bolt, real name Blackagar Boltagon, is a good-aligned male Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in Attilan, Atlantic Ocean and first appearing in Fantastic Four #45 (December, 1965). He works as a King of the Inhumans and is affiliated with Inhumans, Avenging Host, Gods, Crazy Gang and Avengers. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 95 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Flight, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 100 for speed, 85 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 11.
