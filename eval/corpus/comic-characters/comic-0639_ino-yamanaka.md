# Ino Yamanaka

Ino Yamanaka is a good-aligned female Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Volume #4, Naruto Chapter #34. She works as a Leaf Village Ninja. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Absorption, Jump, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
