# Giganta

Giganta, real name Doris Zuel, is a bad-aligned female character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Wonder Woman (Volume 1) #9 (1944). She works as a Criminal, former Scientist, Professor at Ivy University and is affiliated with The Society, Secret Six and Female Furies. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 6250 cm tall and weighs 630 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Intelligence, Size Changing and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 25 for speed, 85 for durability and 40 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
