# Franklin Richards

Franklin Richards, real name Franklin Richards, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York, New York and first appearing in Fantastic Four Annual #6. He works as a Student and is affiliated with Future Foundation, Gods, Power Pack, Generation X and Daydreamers. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 142 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Force Fields, Illusions, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control Resistance, Reality Warping, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 15 for speed, 100 for durability and 50 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 54.
