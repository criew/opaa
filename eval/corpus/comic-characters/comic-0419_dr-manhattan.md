# Dr Manhattan

Dr Manhattan, real name Jonathan Osterman, is a good-aligned male Human / Cosmic character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Watchmen #1 (September, 1986). He works as a Scientist and is affiliated with Watchmen and The Crimebusters. He has White eyes and No Hair hair. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Force Fields, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Size Changing, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 40 for speed, 100 for durability and 45 for combat, giving an overall score of 124.
