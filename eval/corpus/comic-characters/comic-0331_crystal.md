# Crystal

Crystal, real name Crystallia Amaquelin, is a good-aligned female Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in Attilan and first appearing in Fantastic Four #45 (1965). She works as a Mother, adventuress and is affiliated with Neo-Knights, Inhumans, Fantastic Four and Avengers. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Element Control, Fire Control, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 10 for speed, 15 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 6.
