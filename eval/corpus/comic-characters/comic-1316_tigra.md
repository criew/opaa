# Tigra

Tigra, real name Greer Grant Nelson, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, born in Presumably Chicago Illinois USA and first appearing in The Cat #1. She works as a Laboratory assistant, model, adventurer, NYC police and is affiliated with Avengers Resistance, Avengers West Coast and Avengers. She has Green eyes and Auburn hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Enhanced Senses, Jump, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 55 for speed, 40 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 6.
