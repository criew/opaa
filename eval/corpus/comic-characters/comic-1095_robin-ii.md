# Robin II

Robin II, real name Jason Todd, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Detective Comics #524. He is affiliated with Challengers From Beyond and Batman Family. He has Blue eyes and Red hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master.
