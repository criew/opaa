# Nightwing (Arkham)

Nightwing (Arkham), real name Dick Grayson, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Arkham Origins. He works as a (Former) Vigilante/Police Officer. He has White eyes and Black hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Electrokinesis, Intelligence, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 80 for speed, 45 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
