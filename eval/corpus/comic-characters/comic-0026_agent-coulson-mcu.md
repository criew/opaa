# Agent Coulson (MCU)

Agent Coulson (MCU), real name Phillip J. Coulson, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in America and first appearing in Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D. He works as a S.H.I.E.L.D agent. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Endurance, Intelligence, Reflexes, Stealth, Super Speed, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
