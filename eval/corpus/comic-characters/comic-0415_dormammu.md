# Dormammu

Dormammu, real name Dormammu, is a bad-aligned male Cosmic Entity character created by Marvel Comics, born in Faltine Dimension and first appearing in Strange Tales #126 (1964). He works as a Despot, conqueror and is affiliated with Gods, Demons and Titans. He has Yellow eyes and is bald. His notable abilities include Dimensional Travel, Energy Absorption, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Magic, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 142.
