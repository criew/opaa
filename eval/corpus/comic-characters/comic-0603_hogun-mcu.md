# Hogun (MCU)

Hogun (MCU), real name Hogun, is a good-aligned male Alien character created by Marvel Comics, born in Vanaheim and first appearing in Thor (2011). He works as an Adventurer, Warrior. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 58 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 60 for speed, 75 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
