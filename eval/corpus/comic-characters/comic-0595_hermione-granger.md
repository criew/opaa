# Hermione Granger

Hermione Granger, real name Hermione Granger, is a good-aligned female Human character created by J. K. Rowling, first appearing in Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone. She works as a Department for the Regulation and Control of Magical Creatures, Deputy Head of the Department of Magical Law Enforcement, Minister for Magic, Witch and is affiliated with Gryffindor. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Force Fields, Intelligence, Magic, Shapeshifting, Telekinesis and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 5 for strength, 25 for speed, 20 for durability and 45 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 15.
