# Lex Luthor (Injustice)

Lex Luthor (Injustice), real name Alexander Luthor, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Metropolis and first appearing in Injustice: Gods Among Us. He has Green eyes and No Hair hair and stands 208 cm tall and weighs 268 kg.
