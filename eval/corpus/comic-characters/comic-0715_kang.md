# Kang

Kang, real name Nathaniel Richards, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Other Earth, 31st century and first appearing in (As Rama-Tut) Fantastic Four #19 (1963); (as Kang) Avengers #8 (1964). He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 104 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Electrokinesis, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Longevity, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 60 for speed, 70 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 19.
