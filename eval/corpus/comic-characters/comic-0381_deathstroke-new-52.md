# Deathstroke (New 52)

Deathstroke (New 52), real name Slade Wilson, is a neutral-aligned male Human / Altered character created by DC Comics, born in America and first appearing in Deathstroke Vol 2 #1. He works as a Criminal, Mercenary, Assassin. He has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 45 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
