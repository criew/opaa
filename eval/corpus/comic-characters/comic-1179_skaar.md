# Skaar

Skaar is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Sakaar and first appearing in World War Hulk #5, 2007. He works as a Warrior and is affiliated with The Runaways, Dark Avengers and Thunderbolts. He has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 180 kg.
