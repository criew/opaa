# Will Solace

Will Solace, real name Will Solace, is a good-aligned male Demi-God character, first appearing in Percy Jackson And The Lightning Thief. He works as a Healer, Counselor, Archer, Demigod Warrior. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 34 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina and Weapons Master.
