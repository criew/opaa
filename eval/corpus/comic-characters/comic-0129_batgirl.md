# Batgirl

Batgirl, real name Barbara Gordon, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Secret Origins Vol 2 #20. She works as a Detective and is affiliated with Birds of Prey and Batman Family. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 57 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
