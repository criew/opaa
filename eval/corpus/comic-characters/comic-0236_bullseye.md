# Bullseye

Bullseye, real name Lester, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Daredevil #131 (March, 1976). He works as an Assassin, professional criminal, former mercenary and is affiliated with Dark Avengers, A.R.M.O.R and Thunderbolts. He has blue eyes and blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Marksmanship and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 70 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
