# Harbinger

Harbinger, real name Lyla Michaels, is a good-aligned female character created by DC Comics, first appearing in New Teen Titans Annual #2. She works as a Historian. She has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 58 kg. Her notable abilities include Electrokinesis, Element Control, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Reflexes, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 45 for strength, 60 for speed, 60 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 14.
