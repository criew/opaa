# Metallo

Metallo, real name John Corben, is a bad-aligned male Android character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Superman Vol 2 #1 (January, 1987). He works as a Professional criminal and is affiliated with Superman Revenge Squad. He has Green eyes and Brown hair and stands 196 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Beams, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 25 for speed, 95 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
