# Lagoon Boy

Lagoon Boy is a good-aligned male Atlantean character created by DC Comics, born in Atlantis and first appearing in Aquaman Vol 5 #50. He works as an Adventurer, Vigilante and is affiliated with Young Justice. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 249 cm tall and weighs 1800 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Invulnerability, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 40 for speed, 80 for durability and 50 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
