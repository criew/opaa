# Cannonball

Cannonball, real name Samuel Zachery Guthrie, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Cumberland County, Kentucky and first appearing in Marvel Graphic Novel #4 (1982). He works as an Adventurer, peacekeeper, former student, mercenary, miner and is affiliated with Neo-Knights, Ultimate X-Men, X-Men, X-Force and New Mutants. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Absorption, Flight, Immortality and Super Speed. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 65 for speed, 100 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
