# Shades (MCU)

Shades (MCU), real name Herman Enrique Salazar Alvarez, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Puerto Rico and first appearing in Luke Cage Season 1 episode 1. He works as a Hitman, Gangster, Criminal. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 78 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 30 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
