# John Pilgrim (MCU)

John Pilgrim (MCU), real name Robert Pilgrim, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Punisher Season 2: Episode 1. He works as a Hitman. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 80 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 45 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
