# Volstagg (MCU)

Volstagg (MCU), real name Volstagg, is a good-aligned male Asgardian character created by Marvel Comics, born in Asgard and first appearing in Thor (2011). He works as an Adventurer, Warrior. He has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 641 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 55 for speed, 80 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
