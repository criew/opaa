# Miss Martian

Miss Martian, real name M'gann M'orzz, is a good-aligned female character created by DC Comics, born in Mars and first appearing in Teen Titans Vol 3 #37 (August, 2006). She works as an Adventurer and is affiliated with Young Justice, Justice Legion Alpha and Teen Titans. She has Red eyes and Red hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Energy Blasts, Flight, Force Fields, Invulnerability, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis and Telepathy. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 60 for speed, 100 for durability and 45 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
