# Avatar Korra

Avatar Korra, real name Korra, is a good-aligned male Human character, born in Southern Water Tribe and first appearing in The Legend of Korra: The Art of The Animated Series #1 - Book One: Air. He works as an Avatar, Pro-bender. He has Green / Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Element Control, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 60 for speed, 90 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
