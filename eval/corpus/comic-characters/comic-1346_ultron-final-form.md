# Ultron (Final Form)

Ultron (Final Form) is a bad-aligned Android character created by Marvel Comics, born in Cresskill, New Jersey and first appearing in Avengers Volume 4, #4. They work as a Mass murderer, Conqueror. They have Red eyes.
