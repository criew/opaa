# Queen Hippolyta

Queen Hippolyta, real name Hippolyta, is a good-aligned female Amazon character created by DC Comics, born in Themyscira and first appearing in Wonder Woman Vol 2 #1. She is affiliated with Justice Society of America. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Jump, Longevity, Magic, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 55 for speed, 65 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 12.
