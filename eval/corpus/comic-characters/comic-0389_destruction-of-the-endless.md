# Destruction Of The Endless

Destruction Of The Endless, real name Destruction, is a neutral-aligned male Cosmic Entity character created by DC Comics, first appearing in The Sandman Special #1 - The Song of Orpheus. He works as a Personification of Destruction and Creation. He has Brown eyes and Red hair. His notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Energy Beams, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Matter Manipulation, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy, Teleportation and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 112.
