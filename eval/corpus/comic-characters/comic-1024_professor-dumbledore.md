# Professor Dumbledore

Professor Dumbledore, real name Albus Dumbledore, is a good-aligned male Human character created by J. K. Rowling, born in Mould-on-the-Wold, England and first appearing in Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone. He works as a Headmaster at Hogwarts School and is affiliated with Gryffindor. He has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Fire Control, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Magic, Reality Warping, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 5 for strength, 65 for speed, 65 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 31.
