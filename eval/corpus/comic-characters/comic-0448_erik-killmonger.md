# Erik Killmonger

Erik Killmonger, real name N'Jadaka, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Oakland, California and first appearing in Jungle Action #6 (September, 1973). He works as a Tribal leader, would-be conqueror. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 65 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
