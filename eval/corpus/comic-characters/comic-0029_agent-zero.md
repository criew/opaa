# Agent Zero

Agent Zero, real name Christoph Nord, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Unrevealed location in former East Germany. He works as a Mercenary, former government operative, freedom fighter and is affiliated with A.R.M.O.R, Wrecking Crew and Team X. He stands 191 cm tall and weighs 104 kg.
