# Makkari

Makkari, real name Makkari, is a good-aligned male Eternal character created by Marvel Comics, born in Olympia, Greece and first appearing in ETERNALS Vol. 1 #5. He works as an Amateur technologist, Mechanic. He has Strawberry Blond hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Reflexes, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Teleportation and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 100 for speed, 85 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 46.
