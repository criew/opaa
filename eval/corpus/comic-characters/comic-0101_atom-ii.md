# Atom II

Atom II, real name Ray Palmer, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Showcase #34. He works as a Physics Professor and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha, Superfriends, Suicide Squad and Justice League. He has Brown eyes and Auburn hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Flight, Size Changing and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 45 for durability and 60 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
