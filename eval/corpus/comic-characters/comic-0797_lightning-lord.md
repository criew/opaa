# Lightning Lord

Lightning Lord, real name Mekt Ranzz, is a bad-aligned male character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Supergirl and the Legion of Super-Heroes #25 (Feb. 2007). He has Blue eyes and Red hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 95 kg. His notable abilities include Electrokinesis and Energy Absorption. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 40 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
