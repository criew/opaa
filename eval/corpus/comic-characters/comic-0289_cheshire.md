# Cheshire

Cheshire, real name Jade Nguyen, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Vietnam and first appearing in New Teen Titans Annual #2. She works as an Assassin, Mercenary and is affiliated with Titans, League of Assassins and Secret Six. She has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
