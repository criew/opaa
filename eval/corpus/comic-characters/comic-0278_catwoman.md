# Catwoman

Catwoman, real name Selina Kyle, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Detective Comics #569. She works as a Thief and is affiliated with Batman Family, The Outsiders, Birds of Prey, Injustice League, Secret Society of Super-Villains and Female Furies. She has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 6.
