# Gaara

Gaara, real name Gaara, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Sunagakure and first appearing in Naruto, Volume 4, Chapter 35. He works as a Fifth Kazekage, Jinchūriki. He has Blue / White eyes and Red hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Electrokinesis, Flight, Force Fields, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 65 for speed, 95 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 13.
