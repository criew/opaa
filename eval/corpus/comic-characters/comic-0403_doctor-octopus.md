# Doctor Octopus

Doctor Octopus, real name Otto Octavius, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Schenectady, New York and first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #3 (July 1963). He works as a Former atomic-research consultant, criminal mastermind and is affiliated with The FIRM, Sinister Six, Avengers and HYDRA. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 110 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
