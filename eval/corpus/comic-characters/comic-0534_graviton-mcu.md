# Graviton (MCU)

Graviton (MCU), real name Glenn Talbot, is a bad-aligned male Metahuman character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Season 1: Episode 18. He works as a Brigadier General in the U.S. Air Force. He has Hazel eyes and Grey hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 85 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
