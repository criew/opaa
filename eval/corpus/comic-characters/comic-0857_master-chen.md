# Master Chen

Master Chen, real name Chen, is a bad-aligned male Human character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in The Invitation. He works as a Leader of the Anacondrai Cult. He has Red eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 15 for durability and 20 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
