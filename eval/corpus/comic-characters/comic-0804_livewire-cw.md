# Livewire (CW)

Livewire (CW), real name Leslie Willis, is a neutral-aligned female Metahuman character, born in Earth-38 and first appearing in Supergirl Season 1: Episode 5. She works as a formery Shock Jock at Catco Worldwide Media. She has Blue eyes and Grey hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Electrokinesis, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Marksmanship and Super Speed.
