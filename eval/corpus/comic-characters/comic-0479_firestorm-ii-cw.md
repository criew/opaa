# Firestorm II (CW)

Firestorm II (CW), real name Jefferson Jackson, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in The Flash Season 2: Episode 4. He works as a Mechanic, Vigilante, Adventurer. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Marksmanship, Matter Manipulation, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 80 for speed, 70 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
