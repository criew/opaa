# Armor

Armor, real name Hisako Ichiki, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Astonishing X-Men #4 (2004). She works as an Adventurer, student and is affiliated with X-Men. She has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 10 for speed, 80 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 6.
