# Unicron

Unicron, real name Unicron, is a bad-aligned male God / Eternal character created by Hasbro, first appearing in The Transformers: The Movie. He works as a God, Devourer of Worlds, God of Chaos. He has Green eyes and No Hair hair. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Dimensional Travel, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 146.
