# Ronin (MCU)

Ronin (MCU), real name Clint Barton, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in United States of America and first appearing in Thor (2011). He works as an Adventurer. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 40 for speed, 40 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
