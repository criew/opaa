# White Wolf (MCU)

White Wolf (MCU), real name James Buchanan Barnes, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Brooklyn, New York City and first appearing in Captain America: First Vengeance Vol 1 #1. He works as a former Assassin, Soldier, Boxer. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 117 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 60 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
