# Peggy Carter (MCU)

Peggy Carter (MCU), real name Margaret Carter, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Hampstead, England and first appearing in Captain America: First Vengeance Vol 1 #3. She works as a S.H.I.E.L.D.'s agent. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 8.
