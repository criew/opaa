# Jonah Hex (CW)

Jonah Hex (CW), real name Jonah Hex, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in Legends of Tomorrow Season 1: Episode 11. He works as a Bounty Hunter, Sheriff. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 35 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
