# Jerome Valeska

Jerome Valeska, real name Jerome Valeska, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Gotham S1 Episode 16. He works as an Anarchist, Criminal, Circus Performer. He has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina and Weapons Master.
