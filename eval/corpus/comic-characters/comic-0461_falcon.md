# Falcon

Falcon, real name Sam Wilson, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York, New York and first appearing in Captain America #117 (September, 1969). He works as a Crimefighter, (former) freelance artist and is affiliated with The Mighty Avengers, Marvel Knights, Avengers and Heroes For Hire. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 108 kg. His notable abilities include Flight and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 50 for speed, 30 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
