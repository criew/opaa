# Silverclaw

Silverclaw, real name Maria de Guadalupe Santiago, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, born in The village of Kamekeri, Costa Verde and first appearing in Avengers #8 (1998). She works as an University student, adventurer. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Shapeshifting, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 40 for combat, giving an overall score of 4.
