# Luke Cage

Luke Cage, real name Luke Cage, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Harlem, New York City and first appearing in Hero for Hire #1 (1972). He works as an Adventurer, bar owner, bodyguard, mercenary; formerly theater owner, private investigator, various odd jobs and is affiliated with Mighty Avengers, New Avengers, Defenders, Secret Defenders, Marvel Knights, Heroes For Hire, Weapon X, The Six, Thunderbolts and Avengers. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 191 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Invulnerability, Jump, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 30 for speed, 95 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
