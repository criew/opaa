# Captain Atom

Captain Atom, real name Nathaniel Adam, is a good-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Captain Atom #1. He works as a Former Soldier and is affiliated with Justice League Europe, United States Air Force, Justice League and Monarch's Army. He has Blue eyes and Silver hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Force Fields, Immortality, Invulnerability, Self-Sustenance, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 85 for speed, 90 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 37.
