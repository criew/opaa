# Beast Boy

Beast Boy, real name Garfield Mark Logan, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Doom Patrol #99 (November, 1965). He works as an Adventurer, Struggling Actor and is affiliated with Ravagers, Teen Titans, Young Justice, Teen Titans West, New Teen Titans and Doom Patrol. He has Green eyes and Green hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Flight, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 50 for speed, 70 for durability and 40 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
