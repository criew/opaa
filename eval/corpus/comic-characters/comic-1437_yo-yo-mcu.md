# Yo-yo (MCU)

Yo-yo (MCU), real name Elena Rodriguez, is a good-aligned female Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Season 3: Episode 11. She works as a Museum Clerk, S.H.I.E.L.D. Agent. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Endurance, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 100 for speed, 40 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
