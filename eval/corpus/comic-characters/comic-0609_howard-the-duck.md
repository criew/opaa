# Howard The Duck

Howard The Duck, real name Howard, is a good-aligned male Animal character created by Marvel Comics, born in New Stork, New Stork, Duckworld and first appearing in FEAR #19. He works as a Many and various, including former candidate for the United States presidency but usually unemployed and is affiliated with Marvel Knights. He has Brown eyes and Yellow hair and stands 79 cm tall and weighs 18 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 40 for speed, 40 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
