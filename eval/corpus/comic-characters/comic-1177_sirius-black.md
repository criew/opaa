# Sirius Black

Sirius Black, real name Sirius Black, is a good-aligned male Human character created by J. K. Rowling, first appearing in Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban. He is affiliated with Gryffindor. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Fire Control, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Magic, Reality Warping, Shapeshifting, Stealth, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 20 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 17.
