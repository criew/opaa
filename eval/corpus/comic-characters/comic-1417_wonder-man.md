# Wonder Man

Wonder Man, real name Simon Williams, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Paterson, New Jersey and first appearing in AVENGERS #9. He works as a Former industrialist, now stuntman, actor, adventurer and is affiliated with Mighty Avengers, Masters of Evil, Avengers West Coast and Avengers. He has Red eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 171 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Blasts, Flight, Invulnerability, Longevity, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 55 for speed, 90 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
