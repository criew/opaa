# Firelord

Firelord, real name Pyreus Kril, is a good-aligned character created by Marvel Comics, born in Xander, planet in Tranta System, Andromeda Galaxy and first appearing in THOR Vol. 1 #225. They work as a Former starship captain, former herald of Galactus and are affiliated with Annihilators, Heralds of Galactus and Guardians of the Galaxy. They have White eyes and Yellow hair and stand 193 cm tall and weigh 99 kg. Their notable abilities include Durability, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Flight and Heat Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, they score 70 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 75 for speed, 90 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, their overall score is 6.
