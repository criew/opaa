# Firestorm

Firestorm, real name Ronald Raymond, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Firestorm #1 (March, 1978). He works as an Adventurer; former employee of Bun 'n Run and is affiliated with Firestorm Matrix, Superfriends, Black Lantern Corps and Justice Legion Alpha. He has Blue eyes and Auburn hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 91 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Flight, Heat Resistance, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 60 for speed, 80 for durability and 40 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
