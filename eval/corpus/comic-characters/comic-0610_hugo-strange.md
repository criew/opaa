# Hugo Strange

Hugo Strange, real name Professor Hugo Strange, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Detective Comics #36 - Professor Hugo Strange. He works as a Psychologist, Professor and is affiliated with The Society. He has Black eyes and is bald and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 45 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
