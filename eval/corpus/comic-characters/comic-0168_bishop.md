# Bishop

Bishop, real name Lucas Bishop, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in A mutant relocation camp in an undisclosed location on an alternate 21st Century Earth and first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #282 (November, 1991). He works as a (current) Adventurer, (former) Commander in the XSE (Xavier's Security Enforcers) and is affiliated with X-Men. He has Brown eyes and is bald and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 124 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Invulnerability, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 25 for speed, 75 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
