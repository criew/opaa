# Killow

Killow, real name Killow, is a bad-aligned male Human character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Snake Jaguar /70642 Killow vs. Samurai X. He works as a Painter. He has Black eyes and is bald and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 149 kg. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
