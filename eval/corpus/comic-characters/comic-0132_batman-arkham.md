# Batman (Arkham)

Batman (Arkham), real name Bruce Wayne, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Batman Arkham Asylum. He works as a Crime fighter. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Telepathy Resistance, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 55 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 12.
