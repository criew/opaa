# Mammoth

Mammoth, real name Baran Flinders, is a bad-aligned male character created by DC Comics, born in Australia and first appearing in New Teen Titans #3. He is affiliated with The Society and H.I.V.E. He has Blue eyes and Red hair and stands 196 cm tall and weighs 135 kg. His notable abilities include Fire Resistance, Jump, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 75 for speed, 70 for durability and 40 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
