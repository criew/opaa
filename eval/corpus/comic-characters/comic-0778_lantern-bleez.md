# Lantern Bleez

Lantern Bleez is a neutral-aligned female Alien character created by DC Comics, born in Havania and first appearing in Final Crisis: Rage of the Red Lanterns #1 - Prologue: Blood Feud. She works as a Red Lantern and is affiliated with Red Lantern Corps. She has Yellow eyes and Black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 62 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Fire Control, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 70 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 60 for speed, 50 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 12.
