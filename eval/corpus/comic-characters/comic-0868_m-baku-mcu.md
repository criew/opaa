# M'baku (MCU)

M'baku (MCU), real name M'Baku, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Wakanda and first appearing in Black Panther (2018). He works as a Tribe Leader. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 196 cm tall and weighs 113 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 4.
