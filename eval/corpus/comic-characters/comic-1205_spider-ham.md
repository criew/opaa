# Spider-Ham

Spider-Ham, real name Peter Porker, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Basement, Porker residence and first appearing in Marvel Tails Starring Peter Porker the Spectacular Spider-Ham #1 (November, 1983). He works as an Adventurer, photographer, student, vigilante; former circus performer and is affiliated with The Spider Society. He has Black eyes and Brown hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 43 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 60 for speed, 55 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
