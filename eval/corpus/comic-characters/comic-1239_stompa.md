# Stompa

Stompa is a bad-aligned female New God character created by DC Comics, born in Apokolips and first appearing in Mister Miracle #6. She works as a Soldier and is affiliated with Female Furies. She has Strawberry Blond hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 149 kg. Her notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Invulnerability, Reflexes, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 85 for speed, 100 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 20.
