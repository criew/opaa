# Birdman

Birdman, real name Ray Randal, is a good-aligned male God / Eternal character created by Hanna-Barbera, first appearing in Birdman and the Galaxy Trio (1967). His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Force Fields, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 65 for speed, 55 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
