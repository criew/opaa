# Vampire Batman

Vampire Batman, real name Bruce Wayne, is a good-aligned male Vampire character, first appearing in Batman and Dracula: Red Rain (1991). He works as a Vigilante, Serial Killer, formerly Vampire Hunter, Businessman. He has Red eyes and Brown hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 95 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 23.
