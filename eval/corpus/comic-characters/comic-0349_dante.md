# Dante

Dante, real name Dante Sparda, is a neutral-aligned male character created by Capcom, first appearing in Devil May Cry. He works as a Paranomal Investigator, Mercenary, Vigilante Devil Hunter. He has Blue / White eyes and White hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 113 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 90 for speed, 95 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
