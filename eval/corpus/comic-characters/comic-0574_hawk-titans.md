# Hawk (Titans)

Hawk (Titans), real name Hank Hall, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Washington, D.C and first appearing in Titans Season 1: Episode 2. He works as a Vigilante. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 92 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina and Stealth. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 50 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
