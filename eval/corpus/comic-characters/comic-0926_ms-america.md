# Ms. America

Ms. America, real name America Chavez, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Utopian Parallel and first appearing in Vengeance #1. He works as an Interdimensional adventurer. He has Black eyes and Brown hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 70 for speed, 85 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
