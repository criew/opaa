# Mongul

Mongul, real name Mongul II, is a bad-aligned male Alien character created by DC Comics, born in Debstam IV and first appearing in Showcase '95 #8. He works as a Tyrant. He has White eyes and is bald and stands 257 cm tall and weighs 605 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Teleportation and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 70 for speed, 100 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 17.
