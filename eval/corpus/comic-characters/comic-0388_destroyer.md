# Destroyer

Destroyer is a bad-aligned male Robot character created by Marvel Comics, born in Asgard and first appearing in Journey into Mystery #118 (1965). He is affiliated with National Force and Heralds of Galactus. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 290 cm tall and weighs 383 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 70 for speed, 100 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 14.
