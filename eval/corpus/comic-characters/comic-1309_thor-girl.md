# Thor Girl

Thor Girl, real name Tarene, is a good-aligned female Asgardian character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Thor, Volume 2, #22; (as Thor Girl) Thor, Volume 2, #33. She is affiliated with Thor Corps. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 143 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Immortality, Invulnerability, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 70 for speed, 85 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 12.
