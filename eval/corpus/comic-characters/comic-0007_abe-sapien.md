# Abe Sapien

Abe Sapien, real name Abraham Sapien, is a good-aligned male Icthyo Sapien character created by Dark Horse Comics, first appearing in Hellboy: Seed of Destruction. He works as a Paranormal Investigator and is affiliated with Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense. He has Blue eyes and is bald and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 65 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Strength, Telepathy and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 65 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
