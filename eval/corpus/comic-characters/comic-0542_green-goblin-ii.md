# Green Goblin II

Green Goblin II, real name Harold Osborn, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York City and first appearing in Amzing Spide-Man #31. He is affiliated with Dark Avengers. He has Blue eyes and Auburn hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Intelligence, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 35 for speed, 50 for durability and 25 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
