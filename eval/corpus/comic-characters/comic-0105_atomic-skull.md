# Atomic Skull

Atomic Skull, real name Joseph Martin, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Metropolis and first appearing in Action Comics #670 - Skullduggery. He has Black eyes and is bald and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Flight, Invulnerability, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 60 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 40 for speed, 90 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
