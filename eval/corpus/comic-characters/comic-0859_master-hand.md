# Master Hand

Master Hand, real name Master Hand, is a bad-aligned male Cosmic Entity character created by Nintendo, first appearing in Super Smash Bros. (1999). He works as a God of the Smash Bros. universe Minion of Tabuu (Subspace Emissary; formerly, after he is captured) Minion of Galeem (World of Light; formerly, after he is captured) The Announcer of Smash Bros fight (implied). He has No eyes eyes and is bald. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Element Control, Endurance, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 31.
