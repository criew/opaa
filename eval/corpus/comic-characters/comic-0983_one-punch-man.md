# One Punch Man

One Punch Man, real name Saitama, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, first appearing in One Punch - Chapter 1, Volume 1. He works as a Superhero, Sensei and is affiliated with Hero Association. He has No Hair hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 69 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Enhanced Senses, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Jump, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 60 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 95 for speed, 100 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
