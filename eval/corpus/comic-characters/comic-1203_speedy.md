# Speedy

Speedy, real name Thea Queen, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Starling City and first appearing in Arrow (TV Series) - "Pilot". She works as a Nightclub Owner and is affiliated with Team Arrow and Justice Legion Alpha. She has Green eyes and Brown hair. Her notable abilities include Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 5 for strength, 35 for speed, 20 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 4.
