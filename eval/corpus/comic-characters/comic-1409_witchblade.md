# Witchblade

Witchblade, real name Sara Pezzini, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Witchblade Issue 1. She works as a Detective. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 60 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Blasts, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Magic, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 40 for speed, 90 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 10.
