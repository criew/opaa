# Leonardo (TMNT 2012)

Leonardo (TMNT 2012), real name Leonardo, is a good-aligned male Mutant character, born in New York City (1997), and first appearing in Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: vol 1. (1984). He works as a Ninja, TMNT •leader. He has Blue eyes and is bald and stands 160 cm tall and weighs 70 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
