# Elongated Man (CW)

Elongated Man (CW), real name Ralph Dibny, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in The Flash Season 4: Episode 4. He works as a Private Investigator, Vigilante. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 85 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 40 for speed, 95 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
