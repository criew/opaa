# Invisible Woman (FOX)

Invisible Woman (FOX), real name Susan Storm, is a good-aligned female Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, born in Manhattan and first appearing in Fantastic four. She works as a Hero. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition and Telekinesis. Rated across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 75 for speed, 85 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 13.
