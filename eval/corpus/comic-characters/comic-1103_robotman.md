# Robotman

Robotman, real name Clifford Steele, is a male character created by DC Comics, born in Brooklyn, New York and first appearing in My Greatest Adventure #80. He works as an Adventurer, former International Sports Champion, International Race Car Driver. He is bald and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 133 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Endurance, Invulnerability, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 50 for speed, 55 for durability and 60 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
