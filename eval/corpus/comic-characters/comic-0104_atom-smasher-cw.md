# Atom Smasher (CW)

Atom Smasher (CW), real name Al Rothstein, is a bad-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-2 and first appearing in The Flash Season 2: Episode 1. He works as a Criminal. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 196 cm tall and weighs 108 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Jump, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 60 for intelligence, 75 for strength, 65 for speed, 85 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
