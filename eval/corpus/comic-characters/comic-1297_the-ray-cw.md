# The Ray (CW)

The Ray (CW), real name Ray Terrill, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in The Flash Season 4: Episode 8. He works as a Rebel, Freedom Fighter. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 70 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Flight, Force Fields, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 70 for strength, 95 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
