# Desaad

Desaad, real name Unknown, is a bad-aligned male New God character created by DC Comics, born in New Genesis and first appearing in Forever People #2. He works as a Criminal, God of Torture and is affiliated with New Gods of Apokolips. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Electrokinesis, Element Control, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Enhanced Senses, Illusions, Immortality, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy, Teleportation, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 45 for speed, 70 for durability and 60 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 18.
