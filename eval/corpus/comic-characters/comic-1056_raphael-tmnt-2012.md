# Raphael (TMNT 2012)

Raphael (TMNT 2012), real name Raphael, is a good-aligned male Mutant character, born in New York City and first appearing in Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Vol.1 (1984). He works as a Ninja. He has Green eyes and is bald and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 65 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
