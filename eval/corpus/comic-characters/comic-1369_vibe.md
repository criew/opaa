# Vibe

Vibe, real name Cisco Ramon, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in The New 52: FCBD Special Edition #1. He is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha and Justice League of America. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 71 kg. His notable abilities include Dimensional Travel, Energy Blasts and Teleportation.
