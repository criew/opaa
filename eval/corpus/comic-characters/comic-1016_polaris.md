# Polaris

Polaris, real name Lorna Dane, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Presumably northern California - not confirmed and first appearing in X-Men #49 (October, 1968). She works as an Adventurer, former U.S. government operative, former graduate student in geophysics and is affiliated with X-Men, X-Factor, Apocalypse's Horsemen and Marauders. She has Green eyes and Green hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Energy Absorption, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Force Fields and Intelligence. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 75 for strength, 40 for speed, 50 for durability and 45 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
