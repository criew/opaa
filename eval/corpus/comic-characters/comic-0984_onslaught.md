# Onslaught

Onslaught, real name Onslaught, is a bad-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in X-Man Vol 1 #15 (May, 1996). He works as a Would-be destroyer. He has Red eyes and No Hair hair and stands 305 cm tall and weighs 405 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reality Warping, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 85 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 82.
