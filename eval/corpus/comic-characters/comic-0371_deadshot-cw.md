# Deadshot (CW)

Deadshot (CW), real name Floyd Lawton, is a neutral-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 1: Episode 3. He works as a Mercenary, Assassin. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 40 for speed, 40 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
