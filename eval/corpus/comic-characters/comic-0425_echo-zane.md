# Echo Zane

Echo Zane, real name Echo Zane, is a good-aligned male Android character, born in Lighthouse prison and first appearing in The Last Resort. He works as a Helper. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Self-Sustenance, Stamina and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 25 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
