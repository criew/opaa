# Percy Jackson (FOX)

Percy Jackson (FOX) is a good-aligned male Demi-God character, born in New York City and first appearing in The Lightning Theif. He works as a Hero. He has Green / Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Senses, Matter Manipulation, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 70 for speed, 70 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
