# Plantman

Plantman, real name Samuel Smithers, is a bad-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in London, England and first appearing in Strange Tales #113 (October, 1963). He works as a Professional Criminal, formerly Gardner and is affiliated with Thunderbolts. He has Green eyes and Grey hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 87 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Invulnerability, Stealth and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 25 for speed, 55 for durability and 25 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
