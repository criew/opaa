# Apocalypse (FOX)

Apocalypse (FOX), real name En Sabah Nur, is a male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Egypt and first appearing in X-Men: Apocalypse. He works as a Conqueror. He has Brown eyes and is bald. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Cold Resistance, Durability, Fire Resistance, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Reflexes, Regeneration, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 80 for speed, 95 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 34.
