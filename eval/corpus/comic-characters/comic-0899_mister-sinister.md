# Mister Sinister

Mister Sinister, real name Nathaniel Essex, is a bad-aligned male Human / Altered character created by Marvel Comics, born in London, England and first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #221 (September, 1987). He works as a Geneticist, Criminal, biologist and mutant-supremacist and is affiliated with Frightful Four, Nasty Boys, Marauders, Weapon X and Third Reich. He has Red eyes and Black hair and stands 196 cm tall and weighs 128 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Energy Blasts, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Longevity, Mind Control, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 45 for speed, 90 for durability and 50 for combat, giving an overall score of 22.
