# Multiple Man

Multiple Man, real name James Arthur, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Los Alamos, New Mexico and first appearing in GIANT-SIZE FANTASTIC FOUR #4. He works as a Private investigator (formerly) Farmer, Adventurer and is affiliated with Brotherhood of Mutants, X-Factor and Wrecking Crew. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 70 kg. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 70 for durability and 60 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
