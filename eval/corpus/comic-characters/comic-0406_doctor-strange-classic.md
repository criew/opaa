# Doctor Strange (Classic)

Doctor Strange (Classic), real name Stephen Strange, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and first appearing in Strange Tales #110 (July, 1963). He works as a Sorcerer Supreme, retired neurosurgeon and is affiliated with Defenders, Neo-Knights, The Mighty Avengers, New Avengers, Ultimate Fantastic Four, New Warriors, Marvel Knights, Secret Defenders, Midnight Sons and Avengers. He has Grey eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Dimensional Travel, Durability, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Force Fields, Illusions, Immortality, Magic, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Regeneration, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 60 for combat, giving an overall score of 120.
