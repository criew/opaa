# Krypto

Krypto, real name Krypto the Superdog, is a good-aligned male Kryptonian character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Adventure Comics #210 (March, 1955). He has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 64 cm tall and weighs 18 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Invulnerability, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 55 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 100 for speed, 90 for durability and 40 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
