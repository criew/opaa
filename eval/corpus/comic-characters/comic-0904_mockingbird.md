# Mockingbird

Mockingbird, real name Barbara Morse, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in San Diego, California and first appearing in Astonishing Tales #6 (June, 1971). She works as an Adventurer; former counter-terrorist agent, private investigator, government operative, S.H.I.E.L.D. agent, biologist and is affiliated with S.H.I.E.L.D., Hawkeye & Mockingbird, Marvel Knights, Avengers West Coast and New Avengers. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Longevity, Stealth, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 40 for speed, 20 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
