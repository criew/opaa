# Red Hulk

Red Hulk, real name Thaddeus E. Ross, is a neutral-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Incredible Hulk #1 (May, 1962)(as General Ross), Hulk Vol 2 #1 (March, 2008) (as Red Hulk). He works as a Lieutenant general in US Air Force and is affiliated with Super Soldiers, Marvel Knights, New Warriors, S.W.O.R.D and Thunderbolts. He has Yellow eyes and Black hair and stands 213 cm tall and weighs 630 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Energy Absorption, Jump, Longevity, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 45 for speed, 85 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 11.
