# Ghost Rider II (MCU)

Ghost Rider II (MCU), real name Robbie Reyes, is a good-aligned male Demon character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Season 4: Episode 1. He works as a Vigilante, Mechanic. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 30 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 16.
