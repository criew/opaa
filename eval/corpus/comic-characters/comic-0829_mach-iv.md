# Mach-IV

Mach-IV, real name Abner Jenkins, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Baltimore, Maryland and first appearing in (Beetle) Strange Tales #123 (1964); (MACH-I) Incredible Hulk #449 (1997); (MACH-II) Thunderbolts #37 (2000); (MACH-III) Thunderbolts #57 (2001); (MACH-IV) New Thunderbolts #1 (2005). He works as an Adventurer; former master mechanic, professional criminal, mercenary and is affiliated with Thunderbolts. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Flight, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 60 for speed, 85 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
