# Azrael (Gotham)

Azrael (Gotham), real name Theo Galavan, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Gotham Season 2: Episode 1. He works as a Businessman, Philanthropist, Chairman of Development; former Mayor of Gotham City. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 74 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 55 for speed, 75 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
