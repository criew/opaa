# Superman (2006)

Superman (2006), real name Kal-El, is a good-aligned male Kryptonian character created by DC Comics, born in Krypton and first appearing in Superman returns. He works as a Reporter. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Element Control, Energy Absorption, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Invulnerability, Longevity, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 17.
