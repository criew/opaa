# Justice

Justice, real name Vance Astrovik, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Giant-Size Defenders #5 (July, 1975). He works as an Adventurer and is affiliated with Avengers Resistance, Avengers and New Warriors. He has Hazel eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Flight, Force Fields, Super Speed and Telekinesis. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 70 for durability and 30 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
