# Thunderstrike

Thunderstrike, real name Eric Kevin Masterson, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in (as Masterson) Thor #391; (as Thor) Thor #432; (as Thunderstrike) Thunderstrike #1. He works as a (as Thunderstrike) Adventurer, crimefighter, (as Masterson) Architect and is affiliated with Secret Defenders and Thor Corps. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 288 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Longevity, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 70 for speed, 85 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
