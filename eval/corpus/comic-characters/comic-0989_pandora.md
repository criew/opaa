# Pandora

Pandora, real name Pandora, is a neutral-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Flashpoint #5 - Flashpoint: Chapter Five of Five. She works as a Guardian of Pandora's Box. She has Blue eyes and Purple hair. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Immortality, Longevity, Magic, Reality Warping, Self-Sustenance, Stealth, Super Speed, Teleportation, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 60 for speed, 80 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 31.
