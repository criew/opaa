# Sandman (SONY)

Sandman (SONY), real name Flint Marko, is a neutral-aligned male Metahuman character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Spider-Man 3. He works as a Criminal. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 76 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Invulnerability, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 75 for strength, 70 for speed, 95 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
