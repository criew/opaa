# Kraven the Hunter

Kraven the Hunter, real name Sergei Kravinoff, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Volgograd (formerly Stalingrad), Russia and first appearing in The Amazing Spider-Man #15 (1964). He works as a Professional game hunter, mercenary and is affiliated with The FIRM, X-Terminators, A.R.M.O.R, Secret Empire and Sinister Six. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 106 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
