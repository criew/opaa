# Harley Quinn (Arkham)

Harley Quinn (Arkham), real name Harleen Quinzel, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Batman Arkham Origins. She works as a Psychiatrist (Formerly), Professional Criminal. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 50 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
