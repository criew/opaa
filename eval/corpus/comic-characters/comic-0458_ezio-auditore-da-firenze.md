# Ezio Auditore da Firenze

Ezio Auditore da Firenze, real name Ezio Auditore da Firenze, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Ubisoft, born in Florence, Florentine Republic and first appearing in Assassin's Creed #1 - Assassin's Creed. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 76 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Manipulation, Illusions, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 40 for speed, 45 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
