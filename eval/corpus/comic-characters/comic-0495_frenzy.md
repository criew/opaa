# Frenzy

Frenzy, real name Joanna Cargill, is a bad-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in X-Factor Vol 1 #4. She works as a Mercenary and is affiliated with Alliance of Evil, Acolytes and X-Men. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 211 cm tall and weighs 104 kg. Her notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 35 for speed, 100 for durability and 50 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
