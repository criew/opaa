# Titania

Titania, real name Mary McPherran, is a bad-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Denver, Colorado and first appearing in Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars #3 (July, 1984). She works as a Professional criminal, mercenary and is affiliated with Frightful Four. She has Blue eyes and Strawberry Blond hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 245 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Endurance, Invulnerability, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 90 for speed, 95 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
