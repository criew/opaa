# Professor X (FOX)

Professor X (FOX), real name Charles Francis Xavier, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York and first appearing in X-Men. He works as a Headmaster, Adventurer, Teacher. He has Blue eyes and is bald and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 67 kg. His notable abilities include Endurance, Illusions, Intelligence, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Telepathy and Telepathy Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 10 for speed, 15 for durability and 60 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
