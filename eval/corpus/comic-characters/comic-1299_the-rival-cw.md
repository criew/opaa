# The Rival (CW)

The Rival (CW), real name Edward Clariss, is a bad-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in The Flash Season 3: Episode 1. He works as a Criminal. He has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 95 for speed, 70 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
