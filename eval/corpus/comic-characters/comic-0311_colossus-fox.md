# Colossus (FOX)

Colossus (FOX), real name Peter Rasputin, is a good-aligned male Mutant character, born in Ukraine and first appearing in X2. He works as an Adventurer, Teacher. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 203 cm tall and weighs 225 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 35 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
