# Dove (Titans)

Dove (Titans), real name Dawn Granger, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in London and first appearing in Titans Season 1:Episode 2. She works as a Vigilante. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 56 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 45 for speed, 40 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
