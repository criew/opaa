# Gorilla Grodd (CW)

Gorilla Grodd (CW), real name Grodd, is a bad-aligned male Gorilla character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in The Flash Season 1: Episode 5. He works as a Leader of Gorilla City. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 270 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Jump, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Strength, Telepathy and Telepathy Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 65 for speed, 80 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 12.
