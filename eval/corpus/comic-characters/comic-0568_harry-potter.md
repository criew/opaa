# Harry Potter

Harry Potter, real name Harry James Potter, is a good-aligned male Human character created by J. K. Rowling, first appearing in Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone. He works as a Head of the Department of Magical Law Enforcement[ and is affiliated with Gryffindor. He has Green eyes and Black hair. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Magic, Reality Warping, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 5 for strength, 20 for speed, 10 for durability and 50 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
