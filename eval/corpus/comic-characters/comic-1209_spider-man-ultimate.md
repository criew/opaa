# Spider-Man (Ultimate)

Spider-Man (Ultimate), real name Peter Parker, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in United States of America, presumably New York and first appearing in Ultimate Spider-Man #1. He works as an Adventurer, vigilante; former student. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Enhanced Senses, Immortality, Intelligence, Regeneration, Stealth, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 60 for speed, 55 for durability and 50 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
