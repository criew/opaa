# Skalidor

Skalidor, real name Skalidor, is a neutral-aligned Animal character, born in Unknown and first appearing in Can of Worms season 1 episode 5 /9450 Epic Dragon Battle. They work as a General of the Constrictai. They have Yellow eyes and are bald and stand 150 cm tall and weigh 45 kg. On a separate overall ranking scale, their overall score is 7.
