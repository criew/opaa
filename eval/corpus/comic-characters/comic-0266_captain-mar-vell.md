# Captain Mar-vell

Captain Mar-vell, real name Genis-Vell, is a good-aligned male Kree character created by Marvel Comics, born in The city of Rad-Nam on the planet Kree-Lar in the Kree Galaxy (Greater Magellanic Cloud) and first appearing in MARVEL SUPERHEROES #18. He works as a Captain In The Kree Space Fleet, Adventurer and is affiliated with Avenging Host, A-Force and Avengers. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 108 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 90 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 20.
