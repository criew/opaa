# Geo-Force

Geo-Force, real name Brion Markov, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Markovia and first appearing in DC Sampler #1. He works as an Adventurer,Politician,Prince,King and is affiliated with The Outsiders, Justice League and Outsiders. He has Green eyes and Strawberry Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 86 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Energy Blasts, Flight, Force Fields, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 50 for speed, 90 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
