# Callisto

Callisto, real name Callisto, is a bad-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in X-MEN (first series) #169. She is affiliated with Morlocks and X-Men. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 74 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Senses, Reflexes and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
