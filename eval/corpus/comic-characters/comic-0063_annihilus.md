# Annihilus

Annihilus, real name Annihilus, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Planet of Arthros, Sector 17A, Negative Zone and first appearing in Fantastic Four Annual #6 (1968). He works as a Conqueror, scavenger. He has Green eyes and is bald and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Flight, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 45 for speed, 55 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
