# Zoom (New 52)

Zoom (New 52), real name Hunter Zolomon, is a bad-aligned male character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Flash Secret Files and Origins #3. He works as a Criminal · former F.B.I. Profiler, Politician and is affiliated with Flash Family. He has Red eyes and Brown hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Electrokinesis, Element Control, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Intelligence, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis and Telepathy. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 100 for speed, 75 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 20.
