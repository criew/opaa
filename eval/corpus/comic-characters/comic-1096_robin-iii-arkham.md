# Robin III (Arkham)

Robin III (Arkham), real name Tim Drake, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Batman Arkham City (October, 2011). He works as a Student, Crime Fighter, Vigilante. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 86 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 30 for speed, 30 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
