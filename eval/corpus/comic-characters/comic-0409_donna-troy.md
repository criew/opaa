# Donna Troy

Donna Troy, real name Donna Hinckley Stacey Troy, is a good-aligned female Amazon character created by DC Comics, born in Themyscira and first appearing in The Brave and the Bold Vol. 1 #60 (1965). She works as a Photographer, Guardian of the Universe Orb, Adventurer, formerly Fate of the Gods, Queen and is affiliated with Teen Titans and Black Lantern Corps. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 60 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
