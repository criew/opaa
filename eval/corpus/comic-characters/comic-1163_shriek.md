# Shriek

Shriek, real name Frances Louise Barrison, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics. She works as a Former partner of Carnage, Carrion (Malcolm McBride), Demogoblin and Doppelganger). Before she became Shriek, she was a rock band groupie and drug dealer and is affiliated with Carnage Family. She has Yellow / Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Flight and Telekinesis. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 40 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 4.
