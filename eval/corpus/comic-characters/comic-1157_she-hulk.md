# She-Hulk

She-Hulk, real name Jennifer Walters, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Los Angeles, California and first appearing in She-Hulk (Vol. 1) #1. She works as a Lawyer, Magistra of the Living Tribunal and is affiliated with Defenders, The Ultimates, Hulkbusters, A-Force, Future Foundation, Fantastic Four, Avengers and Heroes For Hire. She has Green eyes and Green hair and stands 201 cm tall and weighs 315 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 80 for speed, 100 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 19.
