# Rey

Rey, real name Rey, is a good-aligned female Human character created by George Lucas, born in Unknown (likely in Palpatine’s mansion if he has one) and first appearing in Star Wars: Episode VII - The Force Awakens (2015). She works as a Scavenger and is affiliated with Jedi Order. She has Hazel eyes and Brown hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 57 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Jump, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 65 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 25 for speed, 25 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 14.
