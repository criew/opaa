# Killer Croc

Killer Croc, real name Waylon Jones, is a bad-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Batman #357 (March, 1983). He is affiliated with Secret Society of Super-Villains, Living Assault Weapons, Minutemen, Super Buddies, Suicide Squad and The Society. He has Red eyes and No Hair hair and stands 244 cm tall and weighs 356 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 60 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 35 for speed, 90 for durability and 60 for combat, giving an overall score of 4.
