# Constantine (CW)

Constantine (CW), real name John Constantine, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in Constantine Season 1: Episode 1. He works as an Exorcist, Demonologist, Master of the Dark Arts. He has Brown eyes and Blond hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. His notable abilities include Dimensional Travel, Energy Blasts, Fire Control, Illusions, Intelligence, Magic, Reflexes, Stealth and Telekinesis. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
