# Powerboy

Powerboy is a good-aligned male Alien character created by DC Comics, born in Apokolips and first appearing in Teen Titans Vol 3 #38. He works as an Adventurer. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Invulnerability, Longevity, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 60 for speed, 75 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 11.
