# Black Siren (CW)

Black Siren (CW), real name Laurel Lance (Earth Two), is a good-aligned female Metahuman character, born in Starling City, Earth-2 and first appearing in The Flash Season 2: Episode 22. She works as a District Attorney of Star City, Vigilante. She has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 45 for speed, 45 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 8.
