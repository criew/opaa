# Raven (Titans)

Raven (Titans), real name Rachel Roth, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Titans Season 1: Episode 1. She works as an Adventurer; formerly student. She has Blue eyes and Black / Blue hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Element Control, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Illusions, Magic, Super Speed, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, she scores 70 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 30 for speed, 30 for durability and 50 for combat, giving an overall score of 15.
