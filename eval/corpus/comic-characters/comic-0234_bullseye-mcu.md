# Bullseye (MCU)

Bullseye (MCU), real name Benjamin Poindexter, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Portsmouth new Hampshire and first appearing in Daredevil season 3. He works as an Assasin. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 30 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 40 for speed, 45 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
