# Pied Piper (CW)

Pied Piper (CW), real name Hartley Rathaway, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, first appearing in The Flash Season 1: Episode 11. He works as a Physicist. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 82 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Intelligence and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 15 for durability and 65 for combat.
