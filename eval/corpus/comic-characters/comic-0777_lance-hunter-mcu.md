# Lance Hunter (MCU)

Lance Hunter (MCU), real name Lance Hunter, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Season 2: Episode 1. He works as an Intelligence Agent. He has Green eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 67 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 40 for speed, 35 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
