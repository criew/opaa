# Doctor Sivana

Doctor Sivana, real name Dr. Thaddeus Sivana, Sr., is a bad-aligned Human character created by DC Comics, born in fawcett city and first appearing in Whiz Comics #2 (February 1940). They work as a Scientist, Criminal Master mind, Mayor. They have Grey eyes and are bald and stand 168 cm tall and weigh 55 kg. Their notable abilities include Dexterity, Energy Beams, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Magic, Mind Control Resistance, Size Changing, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, they score 100 for intelligence, 0 for strength, 15 for speed, 25 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, their overall score is 14.
