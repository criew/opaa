# Wolverine

Wolverine, real name Logan, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Alberta, Canada and first appearing in Incredible Hulk Vol. 2 #180. He works as an Adventurer, instructor, former bartender, bouncer, spy, government operative, mercenary, soldier, sailor, miner and is affiliated with X-Force, X-Men, Weapon X, Secret Defenders, Ultimate X-Men, Contingency, Cerebro's X-Men, S.W.O.R.D, Alpha Flight, S.H.I.E.L.D, Four Horsemen of Apocalypse, Apocalypse's Horsemen, Avengers, Defenders, Horsemen of Apocalypse and Fantastic Four. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 160 cm tall and weighs 135 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Immortality, Longevity, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Telepathy Resistance, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 40 for speed, 75 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
