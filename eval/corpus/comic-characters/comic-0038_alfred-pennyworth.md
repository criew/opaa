# Alfred Pennyworth

Alfred Pennyworth, real name Alfred Thaddeus Crane Pennyworth, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Batman #16 (April, 1943). He works as a Butler; Caretaker, former Actor; Field Medic; Government Agent and is affiliated with Batman Family and The Outsiders. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 15 for speed, 10 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
