# Danzō

Danzō is a neutral-aligned Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Volume #32, Naruto Chapter #281. They work as a Leader of Root, Candidate Sixth Hokage, Hokage. They have Black eyes and Black hair and stand 168 cm tall and weigh 52 kg. Their notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Illusions, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, they score 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 50 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, their overall score is 14.
