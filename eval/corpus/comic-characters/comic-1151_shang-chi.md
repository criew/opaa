# Shang-Chi

Shang-Chi, real name Shang-Chi, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Special Marvel Edition #15 (December, 1973). He is affiliated with Heroes For Hire. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 30 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
