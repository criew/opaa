# Changeling

Changeling, real name Kevin Sidney, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #35 (August, 1967). He works as a Former substitute teacher, adventurer, subversive and is affiliated with X-Men. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Shapeshifting, Telekinesis and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
