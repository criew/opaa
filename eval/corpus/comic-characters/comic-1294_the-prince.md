# The Prince

The Prince, real name Dastan, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Ubisoft, born in Persia, Babylon and first appearing in Nintendo Power #32. He works as a Prince, Ruler of Persia, General, Warrior, Adventurer. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 84 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 40 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
