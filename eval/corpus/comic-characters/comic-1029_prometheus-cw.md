# Prometheus (CW)

Prometheus (CW), real name Adrian Chase, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 5: Episode 1. He works as a Serial Killer; formerly District Attorney. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 76 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 45 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
