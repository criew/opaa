# Cheetah III

Cheetah III, real name Barbara Minerva, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Wonder Woman Vol2 #7 (Sept 1987). She works as an Archaeologist, Anthropologist and is affiliated with The Society, Injustice League, Minutemen and Suicide Squad. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Jump, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 90 for speed, 80 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 15.
