# Winter Soldier

Winter Soldier, real name Bucky Barnes, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Captain America Vol 5 #1 (January, 2005) (as Winter Soldier). He works as an Adventurer; former SHIELD operative, Assassin (See Note under "Winter Soldier" article), Army Mascot, Student and is affiliated with Thunderbolts, Marvel Knights, All-Winners Squad, Superiour Six and HYDRA. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 117 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 65 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
