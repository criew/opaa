# Vixen

Vixen, real name Mari McCabe, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Zambesi and first appearing in Action Comics #521 (July, 1981). She works as a Former Model and is affiliated with Justice Society of America, Justice Legion Alpha, Justice League of America, Suicide Squad, Birds of Prey and Justice League. She has Amber eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 63 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Force Fields, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 25 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
