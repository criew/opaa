# Hush

Hush, real name Thomas Elliot, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Batman #609 (January, 2003). He works as a Surgeon, Professional Criminal. He has Blue eyes and Red hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Stamina, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
