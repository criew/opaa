# Man-Wolf

Man-Wolf, real name John Jameson, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York, New York and first appearing in AMAZING SPIDER-MAN #1, (as Man-Wolf) AMAZING SPIDER-MAN #124. He works as an Employed by Dept. of Social Services, Queens; former astronaut, test pilot, security chief for Ravenscroft Institute. He has Brown eyes and Auburn hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 45 for strength, 35 for speed, 40 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
