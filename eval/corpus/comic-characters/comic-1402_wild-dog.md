# Wild Dog

Wild Dog, real name Jack Wheeler, is a good-aligned male character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Wild Dog #1. He works as a Mechanic · former U.S. Marine and is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master.
