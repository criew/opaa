# P.I.X.A.L.

P.I.X.A.L., real name Primary Interactive X-ternal, is a good-aligned female Android character, born in Borg tower and first appearing in The Surge/70724 NinjaCopter. She works as a Cyrus borg his helper. She has Green eyes and Grey hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 10 for speed, 25 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
