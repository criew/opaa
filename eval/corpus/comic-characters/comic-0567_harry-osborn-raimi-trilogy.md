# Harry Osborn (Raimi Trilogy)

Harry Osborn (Raimi Trilogy), real name Harold Osborn, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York City and first appearing in Spider-Man (2002). He works as an Owner of Oscorp. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 75 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Flight, Intelligence, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 60 for speed, 60 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
