# Lady Deadpool

Lady Deadpool, real name Wanda Wilson, is a neutral-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Deadpool: Merc with a Mouth #7 (March, 2010). She works as a Mercenary, adventurer and is affiliated with Deadpool Corps. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 71 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Immortality, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 50 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 60 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 16.
