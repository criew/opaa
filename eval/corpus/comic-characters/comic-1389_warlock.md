# Warlock

Warlock, real name Adam Warlock, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in The Beehive, Shard Island, Atlantic Ocean and first appearing in Fantastic Four #66. He works as an Avenger, savior of worlds and is affiliated with Team X and Guardians of the Galaxy. He has White eyes and Gold hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 108 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 80 for speed, 95 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 11.
