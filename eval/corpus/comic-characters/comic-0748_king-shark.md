# King Shark

King Shark, real name Nanaue, is a bad-aligned male Animal character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Superboy Vol 4 #0. He works as a Professional Criminal and is affiliated with Living Assault Weapons, Minutemen, Secret Society of Super-Villains, Suicide Squad and Secret Six. He has Black eyes and is bald. His notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability, Invulnerability, Longevity, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 50 for speed, 90 for durability and 40 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
