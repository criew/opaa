# Shikamaru

Shikamaru, real name Shikamaru Nara, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Volume #4, Naruto Chapter #34. He works as a Hokage's Assistant, Planner for the Shinobi Union, Proxy Commander of Fourth Division of the Allied Shinobi Forces, Head of the Nara Clan, Leaf Village Ninja. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 53 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Element Control, Fire Control, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Telekinesis, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 50 for speed, 75 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
