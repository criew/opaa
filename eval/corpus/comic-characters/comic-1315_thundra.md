# Thundra

Thundra, real name Thundra, is a good-aligned female character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Fantastic Four #129 (December, 1972). She works as a Ruler, warrior and is affiliated with Squadron Supreme and Frightful Four. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 218 cm tall and weighs 158 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 70 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 30 for speed, 65 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 5.
