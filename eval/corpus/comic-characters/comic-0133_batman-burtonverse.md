# Batman (Burtonverse)

Batman (Burtonverse), real name Bruce Wayne, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Batman (1989). He works as a Vigilante, Businessman. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Fire Resistance, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
