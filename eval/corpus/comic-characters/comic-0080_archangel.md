# Archangel

Archangel, real name Warren Kenneth Worthington III, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Centerport, Long Island, New York and first appearing in X-Men #1 (September, 1963). He works as an Adventurer, Chairman & principal stockholder of Worthington Industries, Multi-Millionaire, Heir, former terrorist and is affiliated with Secret Defenders, Apocalypse's Horsemen, X-Force, X-Men and Horsemen of Apocalypse. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Flight, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 60 for speed, 65 for durability and 40 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
