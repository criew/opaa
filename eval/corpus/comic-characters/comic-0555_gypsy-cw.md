# Gypsy (CW)

Gypsy (CW), real name Cynthia, is a good-aligned female Metahuman character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-19 and first appearing in The Flash Season 3: Episode 10. She works as a Collector. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 56 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Energy Blasts, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 45 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
