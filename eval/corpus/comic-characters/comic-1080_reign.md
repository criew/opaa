# Reign

Reign is a bad-aligned female Alien character created by DC Comics, born in Argo City, Krypton and first appearing in Supergirl #5 - Homecoming. She works as a Conqueror. She has Black eyes and Red hair. Her notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Invulnerability, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 95 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 12.
