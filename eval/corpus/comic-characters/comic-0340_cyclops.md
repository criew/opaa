# Cyclops

Cyclops, real name Scott Summers, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Anchorage, Alaska and first appearing in X-Men #1 (September, 1963). He works as a Leader of mutant race, adventurer, headmaster, former student, radio announcer and is affiliated with Ultimate X-Men, Cerebro's X-Men, Team X, X-Factor, X-Men and Agency X. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 88 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Intelligence and Marksmanship. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
