# Beetle

Beetle, real name Abner Jenkins, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Baltimore and first appearing in Strange Tales #123 (August, 1964). He works as a Former Adventurer, mechanic, professional criminal and is affiliated with Black Cat's Gang, Masters of Evil, America Redeemers and America Redeemers. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Element Control, Fire Control, Flight, Intelligence, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
