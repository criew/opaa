# Red Robin

Red Robin, real name Tim Drake, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Batman #436 (August, 1989). He is affiliated with Teen Titans West, The Outsiders, Justice League Elite and Batman Family. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 56 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
