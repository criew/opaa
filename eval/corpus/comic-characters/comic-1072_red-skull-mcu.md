# Red Skull (MCU)

Red Skull (MCU), real name Johann Shmidt, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Captain America the first avenger. He is affiliated with S.W.O.R.D.. He has Black eyes and No Hair hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 45 for strength, 40 for speed, 40 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
