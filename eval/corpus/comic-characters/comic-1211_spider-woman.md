# Spider-Woman

Spider-Woman, real name Jessica Drew, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in London, England and first appearing in Marvel Spotlight #32 (February, 1977). She works as a Form agent of HYDRA, former bounty hunter, private investigator, adventurer and is affiliated with The Spider Society, Avengers West Coast, S.H.I.E.L.D., New Avengers, Avengers and The Ultimates. She has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 40 for speed, 60 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
