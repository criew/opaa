# Rhino

Rhino, real name Aleksei Mikhailovich Sytsevich, is a bad-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #41. He works as a Professional Criminal ,Henchman of the Russian Mafia and is affiliated with The FIRM, Secret Defenders, Secret Empire and Sinister Six. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 196 cm tall and weighs 320 kg. His notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Durability, Heat Resistance, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 65 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 45 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
