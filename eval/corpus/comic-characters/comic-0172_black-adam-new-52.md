# Black Adam (New 52)

Black Adam (New 52), real name Teth-Adam, is a bad-aligned male character created by DC Comics, born in Egypt and first appearing in Justice league vol 2 10. He works as a King. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Cold Resistance, Durability, Electrokinesis, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Magic, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 90 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 20.
