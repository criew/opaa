# Nightcrawler

Nightcrawler, real name Kurt Wagner, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Somewhere in the Bavarian Alps and first appearing in GIANT-SIZE X-MEN #1. He works as an Adventurer, Teacher and is affiliated with X-Men, Cerebro's X-Men, Contingency, Excalibur, Young Avengers and X-Force. He has Yellow eyes and Indigo hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 88 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Reflexes, Stealth and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 45 for speed, 15 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
