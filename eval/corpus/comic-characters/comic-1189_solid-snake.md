# Solid Snake

Solid Snake, real name David Hayter, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Nintendo, first appearing in Metal Gear (1987). He works as a Soldier, spy, mercenary, musher. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 74 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 16.
