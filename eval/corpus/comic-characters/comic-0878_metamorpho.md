# Metamorpho

Metamorpho, real name Rex Mason, is a good-aligned male character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Brave and the Bold #57. He works as an Adventurer, former Soldier of Fortune and is affiliated with The Outsiders, Outsiders and Justice League Europe. He has Black eyes and No Hair hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Element Control, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 80 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 14.
