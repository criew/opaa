# Robin V

Robin V, real name Damian Wayne, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Batman: Son of the Demon. He is affiliated with Batman Family and League of Assassins. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 137 cm tall and weighs 38 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Longevity, Reflexes, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 15 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
