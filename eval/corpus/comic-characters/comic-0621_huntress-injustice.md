# Huntress (Injustice)

Huntress (Injustice), real name Helena Rosa Bertinelli, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Injustice Gods Among Us Part 6. She works as an Insurgency. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
