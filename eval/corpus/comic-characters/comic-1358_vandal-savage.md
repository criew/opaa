# Vandal Savage

Vandal Savage, real name Vander Adg, is a male Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Green Lantern #10 - The Man Who Wanted the World!. He works as a conqueror and is affiliated with Demon Knights. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 100 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
