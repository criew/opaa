# Goblin Force

Goblin Force is a bad-aligned God / Eternal character created by Marvel Comics, born in The center of the universe during the "Big Bang" and first appearing in Mutant X #1 (October, 1998). They work as a Conqueror. They have Yellow / Green eyes and Red hair and stand 168 cm tall and weigh 52 kg. Their notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Magic, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Regeneration, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, they score 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 20 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, their overall score is 169.
