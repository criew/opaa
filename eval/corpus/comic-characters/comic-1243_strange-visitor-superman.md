# Strange Visitor Superman

Strange Visitor Superman, real name Kal-El, is a good-aligned male Kryptonian character created by DC Comics, born in Krypton and first appearing in Adventures of Superman Vol 2 #16 (October, 2014). He works as a Reporter, Adventurer. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 106 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 47.
