# Heart Of The Monster Hulk

Heart Of The Monster Hulk, real name Bruce Banner, is a good-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, born in Dayton, Ohio and first appearing in The Incredible Hulks - Heart Of A Monster. He works as a Nuclear physicist, Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D. He has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 274 cm tall and weighs 900 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 90 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 41.
