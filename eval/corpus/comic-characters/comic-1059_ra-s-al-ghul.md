# Ra's Al Ghul

Ra's Al Ghul, real name Ra's Al Ghul, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Egypt and first appearing in Batman #232 (June, 1971). He works as an Eco Terrorist, Businessman, Cult Leader and is affiliated with League of Assassins and H.I.V.E. He has Green eyes and Grey hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 97 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Longevity, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 30 for speed, 40 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
