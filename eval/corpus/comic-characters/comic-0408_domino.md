# Domino

Domino, real name Neena Thurman, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Project: Armageddon base, Everglades, Florida and first appearing in X-Force #8 (March, 1992). She is affiliated with Brotherhood of Mutants, X-Men, X-Force and Six Pack. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship and Reflexes. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 5.
