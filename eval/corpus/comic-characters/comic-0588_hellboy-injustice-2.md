# Hellboy (Injustice 2)

Hellboy (Injustice 2), real name Anung Un Rama, is a good-aligned male Demon character created by Dark Horse Comics, first appearing in San Diego Comic Con Comics #2 (August 1993). He works as a B.P.R.D. field agent (1952–2001, 2018–), Luchador (1956). He has Orange eyes and Black hair and stands 211 cm tall and weighs 178 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 38.
