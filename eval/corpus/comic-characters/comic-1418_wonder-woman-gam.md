# Wonder Woman (GAM)

Wonder Woman (GAM), real name Bekka, is a neutral-aligned female character created by DC Comics, born in New Genesis and first appearing in DC Graphic Novel #4 (March, 1985). She works as an Adventurer. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Immortality, Invulnerability, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 70 for speed, 65 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 19.
