# Jemma Simmons (MCU)

Jemma Simmons (MCU), real name Jemma Anne Simmons, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Sheffield, England and first appearing in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Season 1: Episode 1. She works as a Scientist Specialist Field Agent, Biochemist. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Endurance, Intelligence and Marksmanship. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 20 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 9.
