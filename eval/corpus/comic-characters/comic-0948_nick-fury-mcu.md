# Nick Fury (MCU)

Nick Fury (MCU), real name Nicholas Fury, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Huntsville, Alabama and first appearing in Iron Man. He works as a former Director of S.H.I.E.L.D. He has Brown eyes and is bald and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 93 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 45 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
