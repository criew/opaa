# Magneto

Magneto, real name Erik Magnus Lensherr, is a bad-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Unrevealed, probably somewhere in Northern Europe and first appearing in X-MEN Vol. 1 #1. He works as a Currently Revolutionary and Conqueror, formerly Volunteer Orderly, Secret Agent and is affiliated with Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, Brotherhood of Mutants, Hellfire Club and X-Men. He has Grey eyes and White hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 86 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Beams, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Force Fields, Intelligence, Stamina, Super Strength and Telepathy Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 25 for speed, 85 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
