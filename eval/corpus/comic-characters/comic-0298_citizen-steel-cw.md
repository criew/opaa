# Citizen Steel (CW)

Citizen Steel (CW), real name Nate Heywood, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in DC's Legends of Tomorrow Season 2: Episode 1. He works as a Historian, Adventurer, Agent of the Time Bureau. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 170 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Marksmanship, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 35 for speed, 100 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
