# Invincible

Invincible, real name Markus Sebastian Grayson, is a good-aligned male Alien character created by Image Comics, born in Earth and first appearing in Invincible #1. He works as a Hero. He has Grey eyes and Black hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Flight, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 95 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
