# Flash (CW)

Flash (CW), real name Barry Allen, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, born in Fallville,Iowa and first appearing in The Flash 2014. He works as a Forensic Scientist for the CCPD and is affiliated with Flash Family. He has Green / Blue eyes and Brown / Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dimensional Travel, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 100 for speed, 75 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 21.
