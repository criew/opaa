# Hulk

Hulk, real name Bruce Banner, is a good-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, born in Dayton, Ohio and first appearing in Incredible Hulk #1 (May, 1962). He works as a Nuclear physicist, Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D and is affiliated with The Mighty Avengers, Agency X, Contingency, Secret Defenders, Defenders, Marvel Knights, Weapon X, Super Soldiers, The Ultimates, Four Horsemen of Apocalypse, Apocalypse's Horsemen, Avengers and Heroes For Hire. He has Green eyes and Green hair and stands 244 cm tall and weighs 630 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 60 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 85 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 34.
