# Groot

Groot, real name Groot, is a good-aligned male Flora Colossus character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Tales to Astonish #13 (November, 1960). He is affiliated with Guardians of the Galaxy, Marvel Knights and The Mighty Avengers. He has Yellow eyes and stands 701 cm tall and weighs 3700 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Fire Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Regeneration, Size Changing, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 35 for speed, 70 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 11.
