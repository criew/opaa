# Anti-Venom

Anti-Venom, real name Eddie Brock, is a male Symbiote character created by Marvel Comics, born in San Francisco, California and first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #569 (as Anti-Venom). He works as a Vigilante; former journalist for the Daily Globe and is affiliated with Anachronauts. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 229 cm tall and weighs 358 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 65 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
