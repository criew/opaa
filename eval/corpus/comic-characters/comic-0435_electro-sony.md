# Electro (SONY)

Electro (SONY), real name Max Dillon, is a bad-aligned male Metahuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in Endicott, New York and first appearing in The Amazing Spider-Man 2. He works as a formerly Oscorp Electrical Engineer. He has Blue eyes and No Hair hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Flight, Intelligence, Self-Sustenance, Stamina and Super Speed. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 70 for speed, 70 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
