# Wonder Woman (Kingdom Come)

Wonder Woman (Kingdom Come), real name Diana Of Themyscira, is a good-aligned female Amazon character created by DC Comics, born in Themyscira and first appearing in Kingdom Come #1. She works as an Ambassador. She has Blue eyes and Black hair. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Force Fields, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 80 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 23.
