# Shazam

Shazam, real name Billy Batson, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Fawcett City, United States and first appearing in Whiz Comics no. 2 (1940). He is affiliated with Justice League Elite, Young Justice, Justice League and Justice Society of America. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 90 for speed, 95 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 24.
