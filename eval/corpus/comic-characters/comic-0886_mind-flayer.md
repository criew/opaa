# Mind Flayer

Mind Flayer, real name Mind Flayer, is a bad-aligned male character, born in Unknown and first appearing in Stranger things Chapter eight ''The mind flayer'' season 2 episode 8. He works as a Supreme massive inhabitant and ruler of the Upside Down Hive Mind for the Demogorgon and Demodogs. He has Black eyes and is bald. His notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Intelligence, Longevity, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 23.
