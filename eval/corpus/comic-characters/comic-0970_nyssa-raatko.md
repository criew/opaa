# Nyssa Raatko

Nyssa Raatko is a bad-aligned female character created by DC Comics, born in Russia and first appearing in Detective Comics #783. She works as an Assassin and is affiliated with League of Assassins and Justice Legion Alpha. She has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 65 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Longevity, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 20 for speed, 25 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 5.
