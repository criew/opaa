# Cyborg Superman (CW)

Cyborg Superman (CW), real name Hank Henshaw, is a bad-aligned male Cyborg character, born in Earth-38 and first appearing in Supergirl Season 1: Episode 5. He works as an Operative of Project Cadmus. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 185 cm tall. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Energy Blasts, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 85 for speed, 90 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 14.
