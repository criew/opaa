# Weather Wizard (CW)

Weather Wizard (CW), real name Mark Mardon, is a bad-aligned male Metahuman character, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in The Flash Season 1: Episode 1. He works as a Bank Robber. He has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 91 kg. His notable abilities include Electrokinesis, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs and Flight. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 55 for speed, 30 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
