# Dazzler

Dazzler, real name Alison Blaire, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Gardendale, Long Island, New York and first appearing in X-Men #130 (1980). She works as an Adventurer, singer, former actress, model, revolutionary and is affiliated with A-Force, X-Men, Excalibur, Gladiators and S.H.I.E.L.D.. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Force Fields, Illusions, Marksmanship and Stamina. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 60 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 9.
