# Darkhawk

Darkhawk, real name Christopher Powell, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Queens, New York and first appearing in Darkhawk #1 (March, 1991). He works as a Retired Superhero and is affiliated with Secret Defenders, Avengers West Coast, Avengers Resistance, Avengers and New Warriors. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Blasts, Flight, Force Fields and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 70 for durability and 65 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
