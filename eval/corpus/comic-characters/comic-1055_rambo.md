# Rambo

Rambo, real name John Rambo, is a good-aligned male Human character, born in Bowie, Arizona and first appearing in First Blood (Novel, 1972). He works as a United States Army soldier (former). He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
