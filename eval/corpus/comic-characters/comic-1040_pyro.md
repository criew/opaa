# Pyro

Pyro, real name St. John Allerdyce, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Sydney, Australia. He works as a Former government agent, terrorist, bodyguard, novelist, journalist and is affiliated with Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, Brotherhood of Mutants and A.R.M.O.R.. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. His notable abilities include Fire Control. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 10 for speed, 15 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
