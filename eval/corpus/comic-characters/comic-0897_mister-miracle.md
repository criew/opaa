# Mister Miracle

Mister Miracle, real name Scott Free, is a good-aligned male God / Eternal character created by DC Comics, born in New Genesis and first appearing in Mister Miracle #1 - The Murder Missile Trap!. He works as an Escapologist and is affiliated with New Gods of Apokolips and Justice League. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 83 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Illusions, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Matter Manipulation, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 90 for speed, 85 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 23.
