# Spider-Man (Black) (Raimi)

Spider-Man (Black) (Raimi), real name Peter Benjamin Parker, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York City and first appearing in Spider-Man 3. He works as a Freelance Photographer, Vigilante, Adventurer. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 65 for speed, 70 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
