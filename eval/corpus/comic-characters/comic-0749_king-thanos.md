# King Thanos

King Thanos, real name Thanos, is a bad-aligned male Titanian character created by Marvel Comics, born in Titan and first appearing in First Appearance	Last Appearance Thanos Vol 2 #13 (January, 2018) Thanos Vol 2 #18 (June, 2018) (Ceased to exist). He works as a Conqueror, worshiper of Death; former pirate, nihilist, god, knowledge-seeker, King of Titan. He has Silver eyes and Grey hair and stands 201 cm tall and weighs 443 kg.
