# Flash

Flash, real name Jay Garrick, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Hibbardsville, Kansas and first appearing in Flash Comics #1 (January 1940). He works as a Research Scientist, part-time Director of Garrick Laboratories and is affiliated with Justice Society of America, Flash Family, All-Star Squadron, Justice Legion Alpha and Justice League. He has Blue eyes and Brown / White hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Energy Absorption, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Longevity, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina and Super Speed. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 100 for speed, 50 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
