# Sherlock Holmes

Sherlock Holmes, real name Sherlock Holmes, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Dark Horse Comics, born in England and first appearing in A study in Scarlet. He works as a Detective, Boxer, Scientist, Martial artist, Swordsman, Actor, Musician. He has Grey eyes and Black hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 30 for speed, 35 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
