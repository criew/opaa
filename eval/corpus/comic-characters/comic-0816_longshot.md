# Longshot

Longshot is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Longshot #1 (September, 1985). He works as a freedom fighter, stuntman rebel, adventurer and is affiliated with X-Men. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 36 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Telepathy. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
