# Eradicator

Eradicator, real name Eradicator, is a bad-aligned male character created by DC Comics, born in Krypton and first appearing in Superman Vol 4 #2. He is affiliated with The Outsiders and Superman Revenge Squad. He has Black hair. His notable abilities include Element Control, Energy Absorption, Energy Manipulation, Fire Resistance, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Regeneration, Super Speed, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 20.
