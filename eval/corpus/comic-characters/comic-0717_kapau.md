# Kapau

Kapau, real name Kapau, is a bad-aligned male Human character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Only One Can Remain /70755 Jungle Raider. He works as an Anacondrai Cultist. He has Red eyes and No Hair hair and stands 173 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 15 for speed, 10 for durability and 45 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
