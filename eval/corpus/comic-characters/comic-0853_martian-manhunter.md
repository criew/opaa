# Martian Manhunter

Martian Manhunter, real name J'onn J'onzz, is a good-aligned male Martian character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Detective Comics #225 (1955). He is affiliated with Justice Legion Alpha, Justice League, Black Lantern Corps and Justice League of America. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 201 cm tall and weighs 135 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Illusions, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 90 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 26.
