# Acidicus

Acidicus, real name Acidicus, is a neutral-aligned male Animal character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Can of Worms. He works as a Leader Of The Venomari Tribe. He has Green eyes and No Hair hair and stands 7000 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Element Control, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 60 for speed, 50 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
