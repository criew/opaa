# Darth Maul

Darth Maul, real name Maul, is a bad-aligned male Dathomirian Zabrak character created by George Lucas, born in Dathomir and first appearing in Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace (1999). He works as a Sith Assassin, Sith Apprentice, Sith Master and is affiliated with Galactic Empire and Sith Order. He has Yellow eyes and is bald and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 35 for speed, 45 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
