# Sam Winchester

Sam Winchester, real name Sam Winchester, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Wildstorm, born in Kansas and first appearing in Supernatural: Origins #1 - Chapter One. He works as a Hunter,True vessel of Lucifer. He has Green eyes and Brown hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 25 for speed, 50 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
