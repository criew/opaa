# Abra Kadabra (CW)

Abra Kadabra (CW), real name Unknown, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in 64th Century and first appearing in The Flash Season 3: Episode 18. He works as a Time-Travelling Criminal. He has Brown eyes and White hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Dimensional Travel, Illusions, Intelligence, Matter Manipulation and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 30 for durability and 55 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
