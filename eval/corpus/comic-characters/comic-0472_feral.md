# Feral

Feral, real name Maria Callasantos, is a good-aligned character created by Marvel Comics, born in Place of birth unknown and first appearing in NEW MUTANTS #99. They work as a Formerly Adventurer, Terrorist and are affiliated with X-Factor. They have Yellow (without irises) eyes and Orange / White hair and stand 175 cm tall and weigh 50 kg. Their notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Enhanced Senses, Jump, Reflexes, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, they score 70 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 45 for speed, 30 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, their overall score is 4.
