# Lar Gand

Lar Gand, real name Mon-El, is a good-aligned male Daxamite character created by DC Comics, born in Daxam and first appearing in Superboy #89 (June, 1961). He works as a Legionnaire and is affiliated with Legion of Super-Heroes, Justice Legion Alpha and Justice League. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 95 for speed, 95 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 15.
