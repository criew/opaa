# Obito Uchiha

Obito Uchiha, real name Uchiha Obito, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Naruto, Volume 27, Chapter #239. He works as a Leader of Akatsuki, Jinchuriki of the Ten-Tails. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 69 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Electrokinesis, Energy Absorption, Fire Control, Force Fields, Illusions, Invulnerability, Longevity, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Teleportation and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 90 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 65.
