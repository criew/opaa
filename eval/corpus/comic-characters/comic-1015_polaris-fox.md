# Polaris (Fox)

Polaris (Fox), real name Lorna Dane, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in The Gifted Season 1: Episode 1. She works as a Mutant Underground Operative. She has Hazel eyes and Green hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Electrokinesis, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Force Fields, Marksmanship and Reflexes. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 30 for speed, 25 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
