# Bumblebee

Bumblebee, real name Bumblebee, is a good-aligned male Robot character created by Hasbro, born in Cybertron and first appearing in Transformers (July, 1984). He works as an Espionage Director, Spy, Scout and is affiliated with Autobots and Decepticons. He has Blue eyes and is bald and stands 488 cm tall and weighs 3000 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 60 for speed, 75 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
