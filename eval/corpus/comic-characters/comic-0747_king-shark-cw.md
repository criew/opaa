# King Shark (CW)

King Shark (CW), real name Shay Lamden, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character, born in Earth-2 and first appearing in The Flash Season 2: Episode 4. He works as a former Criminal, Marine Biologist. He has Black eyes and No Hair hair and stands 305 cm tall and weighs 270 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Invulnerability, Jump, Mind Control Resistance, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 70 for speed, 95 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 13.
