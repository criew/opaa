# Kylo Ren

Kylo Ren, real name Ben Solo, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by George Lucas, born in Hanna City (Chandrila) and first appearing in Star Wars: Episode VII - The Force Awakens (2015). He works as a Jedi Knight, Dark Jedi, Dark Side Warrior and is affiliated with Jedi Order. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 88 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 30 for speed, 35 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 14.
