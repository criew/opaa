# Caitlin Fairchild

Caitlin Fairchild, real name Caitlin Fairchild, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Deathmate #Black - Black. She works as a Scientist and is affiliated with Ravagers. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 135 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 85 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 17.
