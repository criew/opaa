# Aqualad

Aqualad, real name Garth, is a good-aligned male Atlantean character created by DC Comics, born in Poseidonis, Atlantis and first appearing in Adventure Comics #269 (February, 1960). He works as an Adventurer; Magician, former Sidekick and is affiliated with Justice League Atlantis, Aquaman Family and Teen Titans. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 106 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Beams, Enhanced Hearing, Magic, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 45 for strength, 40 for speed, 75 for durability and 60 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
