# Symbiote Wolverine

Symbiote Wolverine, real name Logan, is a neutral-aligned male Symbiote character created by Marvel Comics, born in Alberta, Canada and first appearing in Spider-Man: Web of Shadows (October 21, 2008). He works as an Adventurer. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 160 cm tall and weighs 135 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 60 for intelligence, 70 for strength, 75 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 16.
