# Nite Owl

Nite Owl, real name Hollis Mason, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Montana and first appearing in Watchmen #1 (September, 1986). He works as a Police Officer, Vigilante, Mechanic. He has Brown eyes and Grey hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 25 for strength, 35 for speed, 45 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
