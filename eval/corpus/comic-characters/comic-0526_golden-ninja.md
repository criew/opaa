# Golden Ninja

Golden Ninja, real name Lloyd Garmadon, is a good-aligned male God / Eternal character created by Lego, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Rise of the Spinjitzu Master. He works as a Ninja. He has White eyes and Blond hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 69 kg.
