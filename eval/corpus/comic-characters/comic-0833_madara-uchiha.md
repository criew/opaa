# Madara Uchiha

Madara Uchiha, real name Uchiha Madara, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, first appearing in Naruto, Volume 41, Chapter 370. He works as a Jinchuriki of the Juubi, Missing-nin, Sage, Co-Founder of Konohagakure, Former Head of the Uchiha Clan. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 71 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Element Control, Energy Absorption, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Force Fields, Illusions, Immortality, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reality Warping, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 66.
