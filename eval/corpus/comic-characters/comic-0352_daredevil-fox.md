# Daredevil (FOX)

Daredevil (FOX), real name Matt Murdock, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in New York City, New York and first appearing in Daredevil season 1 Into the Ring. He works as a Crime fighter. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses and Peak Human Condition. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 80 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
