# Batman (LEGO)

Batman (LEGO), real name Bruce Wayne, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Lego, born in Gotham city and first appearing in Lego Batman Movie. He works as a Superhero. He has White eyes and is bald and stands 3 cm tall and weighs 1 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 25 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
