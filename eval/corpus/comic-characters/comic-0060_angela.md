# Angela

Angela, real name Aldrif Odinsdottir, is a neutral-aligned female God / Eternal character created by Marvel Comics, born in Asgard and first appearing in Spawn #9 - Angela. She works as an Adventurer, hunter, mercenary; Queen of Hel, Loki's bodyguard. She has White eyes and Red hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 216 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 70 for speed, 75 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 15.
