# Krux

Krux, real name Krux, is a bad-aligned Human character, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Dark Island Trilogy, Part 3 (vision)/70626 Dawn of Iron Doom. They work as an Ex-elemental master of time. They have Gold eyes and Grey hair. Their notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Element Control, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Jump, Longevity, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, they score 90 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 90 for speed, 90 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 21.
