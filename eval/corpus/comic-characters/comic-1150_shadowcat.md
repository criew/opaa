# Shadowcat

Shadowcat, real name Kitty Pryde, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Deerfield, Illinois and first appearing in X-Men #129 (January, 1980). She works as a Teacher, Adventurer, former student and is affiliated with X-Men, Ultimate X-Men and Excalibur. She has Hazel eyes and Brown hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Intelligence, Stealth, Telepathy Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 25 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
