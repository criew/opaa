# The Keeper

The Keeper, real name Norrin Radd, is a good-aligned male Alien character created by Marvel Comics, born in Somewhere on Zenn-La, Deneb System, the Milky Way Galaxy. and first appearing in Silver Surfer Annual #4 (July, 1991). He works as an Eon's Protector of the Universe; Spacefaring adventurer and explorer; former would-be world conqueror, minion of Gaea, Earthbound adventurer, scholar; sometimes mistakenly regarded as a messianic figure. He has White eyes and No Hair hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Manipulation, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Illusions, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Matter Manipulation, Mind Control, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Size Changing, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Teleportation and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 152.
