# Hiruzen Sarutobi

Hiruzen Sarutobi is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Volume #1, Naruto Chapter #1. He works as a Hokage, Leaf Village Ninja. He has Black eyes and Grey hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Electrokinesis, Element Control, Energy Absorption, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Illusions, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 50 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 13.
