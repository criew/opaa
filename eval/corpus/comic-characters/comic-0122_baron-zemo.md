# Baron Zemo

Baron Zemo, real name Helmut Zemo, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Leipzig, Germany and first appearing in Captain America #168 (December, 1973). He works as a Terrorist, ruler of Bagalia, 13th Baron of Zeulniz, adventurer, mastermind, would-be world savior with world conquest inclinations; former professional criminal, assassin, engineer, wealthy criminal entrpeneur and is affiliated with Masters of Evil, Avengers, HYDRA and Thunderbolts. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 82 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Endurance, Intelligence, Longevity, Mind Control, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Stamina, Telepathy Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 20 for speed, 65 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
