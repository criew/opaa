# Resurrection Spawn

Resurrection Spawn, real name Al Simmons, is a good-aligned male Demon character created by Image Comics, born in Detroit, Michigan (8th level of Hell after rebirth). He works as a Vigilante; former government agent, assassin. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 211 cm tall. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 75 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 25.
