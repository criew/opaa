# Ant-Man II

Ant-Man II, real name Scott Lang, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Coral Gables, Florida and first appearing in Avengers Vol 1 #181 (March, 1979). He works as an Electronics Technician, and is affiliated with Defenders, Marvel Knights, Heroes For Hire, The Mighty Avengers, Future Foundation and Avengers. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 86 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Size Changing, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 15 for speed, 40 for durability and 30 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
