# Reverse Flash (CW)

Reverse Flash (CW), real name Eobard Thawne, is a bad-aligned male character created by DC Comics, first appearing in The Flash: Pilot. He works as a Scientist and is affiliated with Flash Family. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Electrokinesis and Enhanced Senses. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 80 for speed, 65 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
