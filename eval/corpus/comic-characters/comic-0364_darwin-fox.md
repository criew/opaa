# Darwin (FOX)

Darwin (FOX), real name Armando Muñoz, is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in X-Men: First Class. He works as a Taxi Driver. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Self-Sustenance, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 30 for speed, 100 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
