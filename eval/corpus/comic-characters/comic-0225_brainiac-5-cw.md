# Brainiac 5 (CW)

Brainiac 5 (CW), real name Querl Dox, is a good-aligned male Alien character, born in Colu, Earth-1 and first appearing in Supergirl Season 3: Episode 9. He works as an Adventurer, Vigilante, Technician at the D.E.O. He has Brown eyes and White hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 55 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Flight, Intelligence, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 40 for strength, 60 for speed, 80 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 12.
