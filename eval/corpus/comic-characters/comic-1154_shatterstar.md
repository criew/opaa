# Shatterstar

Shatterstar, real name Gaveedra Seven, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Mojoworld and first appearing in New Mutants #99. He works as a Warrior and is affiliated with X-Force. He has Brown eyes and Red hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 88 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Energy Blasts, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 45 for speed, 65 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
