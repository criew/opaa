# Bushmaster (MCU)

Bushmaster (MCU), real name John Mclver, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Brooklyn and first appearing in Luke Cage Season 2: Episode 1. He works as a Gangster. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 85 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 60 for speed, 70 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
