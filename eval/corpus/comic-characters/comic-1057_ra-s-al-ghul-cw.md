# Ra's Al Ghul (CW)

Ra's Al Ghul (CW), real name Unknown, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 3: Episode 4. He works as a Head of the League of Assassins. He has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dexterity, Durability, Intelligence, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
