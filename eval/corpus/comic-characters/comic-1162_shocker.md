# Shocker

Shocker, real name Herman Schultz, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #46. He works as a Professional thief, hired assassin and is affiliated with Black Cat's Gang, Masters of Evil, Sinister Seven, The FIRM, New Enforcers, Secret Empire, Sinister Syndicate, Sinister Twelve and Sinister Six. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts, Marksmanship and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 70 for durability and 55 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
