# Brother Voodoo

Brother Voodoo, real name Jericho Drumm, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Port-au-Prince, Haiti and first appearing in Strange Tales #169 (September, 1973). He works as a Psychologist Houngan (voodoo priest). He has Brown eyes and Brown / White hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Element Control, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Magic and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 55 for speed, 60 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
