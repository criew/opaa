# Executioner

Executioner, real name Skurge, is a neutral-aligned male God / Eternal character created by Marvel Comics, born in Jotunheim, Asgard and first appearing in Journey into Mystery #103 - The Enchantress And The Executioner!. He works as a Warrior,warlord,co-ruler of Casiolena's realm,Hela's Executioner, and is affiliated with Masters of Evil and National Force. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 218 cm tall and weighs 495 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Invulnerability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 70 for strength, 50 for speed, 90 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
