# Firestorm (CW)

Firestorm (CW), real name Ronald Raymond, is a good-aligned male Metahuman character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in The Flash Season 1: Episode 3. He works as a formerly structural engineer, vigilante. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 78 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Matter Manipulation, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 80 for speed, 70 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 12.
