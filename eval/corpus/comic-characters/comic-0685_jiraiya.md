# Jiraiya

Jiraiya is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Volume #10, Naruto Chapter #90. He works as a Leaf Village Ninja,Writer. He has Black eyes and White hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 86 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 90 for strength, 60 for speed, 85 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 16.
