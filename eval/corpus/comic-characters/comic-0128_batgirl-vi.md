# Batgirl VI

Batgirl VI, real name Stephanie Brown, is a good-aligned female character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Detective Comics #647. As Batgirl in Batgirl (Volume 3) #1. (2009). She works as a College student at Gotham University and is affiliated with Batman Family and Young Justice. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Marksmanship, Stamina and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 6.
