# Caliban

Caliban is a good-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #148. He works as a Morlock, X-Men Member and is affiliated with Morlocks, X-Force, X-Factor, Apocalypse's Horsemen and X-Men. He has Yellow eyes and is bald and stands 203 cm tall and weighs 124 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Absorption, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 35 for speed, 50 for durability and 60 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 5.
