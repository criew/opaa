# Samukai

Samukai, real name Samukai, is a bad-aligned male character, born in Underworld and first appearing in Way of the Ninja. He works as a Leader Of The Skulkin. He has Red eyes and is bald and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Electrokinesis, Element Control, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Fire Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 50 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 40 for speed, 60 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 11.
