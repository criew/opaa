# Lobo

Lobo, real name Lobo, is a neutral-aligned male Czarnian character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Omega Men # 3. He works as an Assassin, Bounty Hunter, Priest and is affiliated with Justice League Elite, Justice League of America, LEGION, First Celestial Church of the Triple Fish-God and Red Lantern Corps. He has Red eyes and Black hair and stands 229 cm tall and weighs 288 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Enhanced Senses, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Strength and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 55 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 19.
