# Starro The Star Conqueror

Starro The Star Conqueror, real name Star-Ro, is a male Alien character created by DC Comics, born in Hatorei and first appearing in The Brave and the Bold #28 - Starro the Conqueror!. He works as a Celestial conqueror, Transgalactic conqueror,. He has Red eyes and is bald. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Energy Absorption, Flight, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Mind Control, Regeneration, Size Changing, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 70 for speed, 100 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 14.
