# Silver Banshee

Silver Banshee, real name Siobhan McDougal, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Ireland and first appearing in Action Comics #595 - The Ghost of Superman. She works as a Professional Criminal and is affiliated with Superman Revenge Squad. She has White eyes and White hair and stands 211 cm tall and weighs 81 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Endurance, Flight, Intelligence, Magic, Marksmanship, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 75 for speed, 75 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 9.
