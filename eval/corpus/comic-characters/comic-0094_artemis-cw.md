# Artemis (CW)

Artemis (CW), real name Evelyn Sharp, is a bad-aligned female Human character, born in Starling City, Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 4: Episode 19. She works as a formerly Vigilante. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 51 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 35 for speed, 30 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
