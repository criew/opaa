# Highfather

Highfather, real name Izaya, is a good-aligned male God / Eternal character created by DC Comics, born in New Genesis and first appearing in The New Gods (vol. 1) #1. He works as a Leader of the New Gods and is affiliated with New Gods of Apokolips. He has Grey eyes and White hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 102 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Force Fields, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Marksmanship, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 90 for speed, 100 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 38.
