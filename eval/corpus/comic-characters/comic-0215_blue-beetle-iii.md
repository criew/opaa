# Blue Beetle III

Blue Beetle III, real name Jaime Reyes, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Presumed to be El Paso and first appearing in Infinite Crisis #3 (2006). He works as a Student and is affiliated with Young Justice, Teen Titans and Justice League. He has Brown eyes and Black hair. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Force Fields, Heat Resistance and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 60 for speed, 80 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
