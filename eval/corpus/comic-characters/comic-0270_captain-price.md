# Captain Price

Captain Price, real name John Price, is a good-aligned male Human character, first appearing in Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare. He works as a British S.A.S Captain, 22nd Regiment Member, Task Force 141 Captain. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 20 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
