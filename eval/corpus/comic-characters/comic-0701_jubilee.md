# Jubilee

Jubilee, real name Jubilation Lee, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Beverly Hills, California and first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #244 (May, 1989). She works as a Student, Adventurer and is affiliated with Cerebro's X-Men, The Runaways, Generation X, X-Men and Alpha Flight. She has Red eyes and Black hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts and Energy Manipulation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 20 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 5.
