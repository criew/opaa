# Penance II

Penance II, real name Robert Baldwin, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Springdale, Connecticut and first appearing in (as Speedball) Amazing Spider-Man Annual #22 (1988); (as Penance) Civil War: Front Line #10 (2007). He works as a Government operative; former adventurer, reality TV star, student, television show intern, engineering intern, lab worker and is affiliated with Thunderbolts. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 89 kg. His notable abilities include Energy Blasts.
