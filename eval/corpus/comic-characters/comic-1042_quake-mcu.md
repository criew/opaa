# Quake (MCU)

Quake (MCU), real name Daisy Johnson, is a good-aligned female Human-Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D season one pilot. She works as a S.H.I.E.L.D agent. She has Brown eyes and Blond hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 74 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Stamina and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 55 for speed, 65 for durability and 50 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 11.
