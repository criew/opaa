# Predator

Predator, real name Yautja, is a bad-aligned male Yautja character created by Dark Horse Comics, born in Yautja Prime and first appearing in Predator (1987). He stands 213 cm tall and weighs 234 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Cold Resistance, Durability, Intelligence, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 25 for speed, 85 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 8.
