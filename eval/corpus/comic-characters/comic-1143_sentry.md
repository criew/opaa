# Sentry

Sentry, real name Robert Reynolds, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Sentry #1 (September, 2000). He is affiliated with New Warriors, Dark Avengers, Mighty Avengers, Super Soldiers, Gods, Horsemen of Apocalypse, New Avengers, Apocalypse's Horsemen and Avengers. He has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 87 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Blasts, Enhanced Hearing, Flight, Force Fields, Immortality, Intelligence, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy and Teleportation.
