# Buffy

Buffy, real name Buffy Anne Summers, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Dark Horse Comics, first appearing in Dark Horse Presents 1998 Annual. She works as a Slayer ; (formerly) Waitress ; DoubleMeat Palace employee ; Sunnydale High Counselor and is affiliated with Scooby Gang. She has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 40 for speed, 70 for durability and 60 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
