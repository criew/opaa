# Elastigirl

Elastigirl, real name Helen Parr, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Dark Horse Comics, first appearing in The Incredibles (Movies, 2004). She is affiliated with Incredible Family. She has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 56 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Invulnerability, Shapeshifting, Size Changing and Super Strength.
