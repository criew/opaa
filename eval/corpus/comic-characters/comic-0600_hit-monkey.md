# Hit-Monkey

Hit-Monkey, real name Unknown, is a neutral-aligned male Animal character created by Marvel Comics, born in Honshu, Japan and first appearing in Deadpool Vol 4 #19. He works as an Assassin. He has Amber eyes and Grey hair and stands 74 cm tall and weighs 14 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 50 for speed, 45 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 7.
