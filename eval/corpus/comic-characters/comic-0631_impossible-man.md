# Impossible Man

Impossible Man is a neutral-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Fantastic Four #11. He works as a Prankster, Hedonist, Student of Earth's popular culture, Progenitor of the new Poppupian race. He has Purple eyes and No Hair hair and stands 193 cm tall and weighs 74 kg. His notable abilities include Dimensional Travel, Self-Sustenance, Shapeshifting, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 95 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 15.
