# Sebastian Shaw (FOX)

Sebastian Shaw (FOX), real name Sebastian Hiram Shaw, is a bad-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in X-Men First Class. He works as a Leader of the Hellfire Club. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 75 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Manipulation, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Longevity, Self-Sustenance and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 75 for strength, 25 for speed, 90 for durability and 50 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 12.
