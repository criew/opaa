# Felix Faust

Felix Faust, real name Dekan Drache, is a bad-aligned male God / Eternal character created by DC Comics, born in Kor and first appearing in Justice League of America #10 - The Fantastic Fingers of Felix Faust!. He works as a Magician, Sorcerer. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Illusions, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Magic, Reality Warping, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 65 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 28.
