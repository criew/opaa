# Jean Grey

Jean Grey, real name Jean Grey, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in X-Men #1 (September, 1963). She works as an Adventurer and is affiliated with X-Men, Cerebro's X-Men, Ultimate X-Men and X-Factor. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Energy Absorption, Energy Manipulation, Force Fields, Illusions, Mind Control, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Telepathy Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 20 for speed, 20 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 11.
