# Hela

Hela is a bad-aligned female Asgardian character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Journey into Mystery #102. She works as an Asgardian Goddess of Death, former Ruler of Hel and Niffleheim and is affiliated with Gods of Esgard and Hell Lords. She has Green eyes and Black hair and stands 213 cm tall and weighs 225 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Illusions, Invulnerability, Longevity, Magic, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 45 for speed, 100 for durability and 45 for combat, giving an overall score of 16.
