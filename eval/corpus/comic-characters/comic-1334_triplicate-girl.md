# Triplicate Girl

Triplicate Girl, real name Luornu Durgo, is a good-aligned female character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Teen Titans/Legion Special #1. She is affiliated with Legion of Super-Heroes. She has Purple eyes and Brown hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Flight.
