# Minato Namikaze

Minato Namikaze is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, born in Konohagakure and first appearing in Volume #27, Naruto Chapter #239. He works as a Hokage, Leaf Village Ninja. He has Blue eyes and Yellow hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 66 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Energy Constructs, Energy Manipulation, Intelligence, Jump, Magic, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Teleportation, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master.
