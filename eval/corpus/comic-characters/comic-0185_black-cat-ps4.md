# Black Cat (PS4)

Black Cat (PS4), real name Felicia Hardy, is a neutral-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Marvel's Spiderman. She works as a Cat burglar. She has Blue eyes and White hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 68 kg. Her notable abilities include Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 65 for speed, 30 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 5.
