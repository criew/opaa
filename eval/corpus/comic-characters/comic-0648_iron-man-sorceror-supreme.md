# Iron Man (Sorceror Supreme)

Iron Man (Sorceror Supreme), real name Doctor Anthony Stark, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Sofia, Bulgaria and first appearing in All-New X-Men Annual #1 (February, 2015). He works as an Adventurer, Sorcerer Supreme, Engineer, Inventor; former Scientist. He has Brown eyes and White hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Dexterity, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Element Control, Endurance, Energy Blasts, Fire Control, Flight, Force Fields, Illusions, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Magic, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 70 for speed, 100 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 60.
