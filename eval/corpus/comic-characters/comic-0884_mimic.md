# Mimic

Mimic, real name Calvin Montgomery Rankin, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Passiac, New Jersey and first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #19. He is affiliated with X-Men and Dark X-Men. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Energy Blasts, Flight, Reflexes, Super Strength, Telekinesis and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 45 for speed, 55 for durability and 40 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
