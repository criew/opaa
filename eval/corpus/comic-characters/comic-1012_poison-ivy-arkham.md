# Poison Ivy (Arkham)

Poison Ivy (Arkham), real name Pamela Isley, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Batman: Arkham Asylum. She works as a Botanist (formerly), Professional Criminal (formerly), Ecoterrorist (formerly). She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Mind Control, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Telepathy and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 95 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 55 for speed, 70 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 11.
