# Captain Boomerang (CW)

Captain Boomerang (CW), real name Digger Harkness, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in Arrow Season 3: Episode 7. He works as a Mercenary; formerly Intelligence Agent. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 180 cm tall. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 35 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
