# Hawkgirl

Hawkgirl, real name Kendra Saunders, is a good-aligned female character created by DC Comics, first appearing in JSA Secret Files #1 (August 1999). She works as an Adventurer, Film School Student, Temp Curator of the Stonechat Museum and is affiliated with Superfriends, Justice League of America, Justice Legion Alpha, Birds of Prey and Justice League. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Dexterity, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Self-Sustenance, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 90 for speed, 100 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 20.
