# Lightspeed

Lightspeed, real name Julie Power, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Richmond, Virginia and first appearing in Power Pack #1. She works as an Adventurer; former student. She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Element Control, Flight, Invulnerability, Super Speed and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 100 for speed, 55 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 9.
