# Saturn Girl (CW)

Saturn Girl (CW), real name Imra Ardeen, is a good-aligned female Titanian character created by DC Comics, born in 31st Century Titan and first appearing in Supergirl Season 3: Episode 7. She works as a Founder and Co-Leader of the Legion of Super-Heroes, Vigilante. She has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 52 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Flight, Force Fields, Illusions, Stamina, Super Strength, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Telepathy Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 35 for strength, 30 for speed, 40 for durability and 75 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
