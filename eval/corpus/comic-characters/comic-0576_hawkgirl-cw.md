# Hawkgirl (CW)

Hawkgirl (CW), real name Kendra Saunders, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Flash season 1. She works as a Vigilante (in secret; formerly) Barista at CC Jitters (formerly) Librarian (formerly; 1958-1960). She has Blue eyes and Blond hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Flight and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 55 for speed, 100 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 8.
