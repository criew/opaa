# Hashirama Senju

Hashirama Senju is a good-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, first appearing in Volume #14, Naruto Chapter #118. He works as a Co-founder of Konohagakure, Hokage, Head of the Senju clan, Leaf Village Ninja. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 73 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Element Control, Energy Absorption, Energy Constructs, Enhanced Senses, Fire Control, Illusions, Intelligence, Jump, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 80 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 26.
