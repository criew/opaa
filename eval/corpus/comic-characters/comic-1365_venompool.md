# Venompool

Venompool, real name Wade Wilson, is a male Symbiote character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in What If? Iron Man: Demon in an Armor #1 (February, 2011). He works as a Conqueror; Adventurer and is affiliated with Evil Deadpool Corps. He stands 226 cm tall. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Immortality, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 65 for speed, 100 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 23.
