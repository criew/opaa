# Dean Winchester

Dean Winchester, real name Dean Winchester, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Wildstorm, born in Kansas and first appearing in Supernatural: Origins #1 - Chapter One. He works as a Hunter,True vessel of Michael. He has Green eyes and Brown hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 25 for speed, 50 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
