# Misty Knight (MCU)

Misty Knight (MCU), real name Mercedes Kelly, is a good-aligned female Human character created by Marvel Comics, born in Harlem, New York City and first appearing in Luke Cage Season 1: Episode 1. She works as a NYPD Detective. She has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 168 cm tall and weighs 59 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 90 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 35 for speed, 35 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 7.
