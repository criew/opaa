# Medusa (MCU)

Medusa (MCU), real name Medusalith Amaquelin, is a good-aligned female Inhuman character created by Marvel Comics, born in Attilan and first appearing in Inhumans Season 1: Episode 1. She works as a Queen of Attilan and is affiliated with A-Force. She has Blue eyes and Red hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 60 kg. Her notable abilities include Durability, Endurance and Stamina. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 30 for speed, 50 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 6.
