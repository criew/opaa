# Hybrid

Hybrid, real name Scott Washington, is a good-aligned male Symbiote character created by Marvel Comics, born in Bedford-Stuyvesant, Brooklyn, New York City and first appearing in Venom Along Came A Spider #2 (as Hybrid). He is affiliated with Guardsmen and NYPD. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 77 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Flight, Jump, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated across attributes, he scores 80 for intelligence, 65 for strength, 60 for speed, 75 for durability and 80 for combat, giving an overall score of 9.
