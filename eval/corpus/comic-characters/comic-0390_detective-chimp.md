# Detective Chimp

Detective Chimp, real name Bobo T. Chimpanzee, is a good-aligned male Gorilla character created by DC Comics, born in Africa and first appearing in Adventures of Rex the Wonder Dog #4 (August, 1952). He works as a Detective, Occult Investigator. He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 109 cm tall and weighs 34 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Immortality, Intelligence, Longevity, Magic, Reflexes and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 60 for speed, 85 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 12.
