# X-Man

X-Man, real name Nate Grey, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in American Northeast of Earth-295 and first appearing in X-Man #1 (1995). He works as a Former shaman, adventurer, freedom fighter and is affiliated with Neo-Knights and X-Men. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. His notable abilities include Flight, Illusions, Mind Control, Telekinesis, Telepathy and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 55 for strength, 55 for speed, 95 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
