# Hydro-Man

Hydro-Man, real name Morris Bench, is a bad-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Bronx, New York and first appearing in Amazing Spider-Man #212 (January, 1981). He works as a Professional Criminal; former crewman of cargo ship and is affiliated with The FIRM, Frightful Four and Sinister Twelve. He has Brown eyes and Brown hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 119 kg. His notable abilities include Invulnerability, Shapeshifting and Size Changing. Rated across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 25 for speed, 80 for durability and 50 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
