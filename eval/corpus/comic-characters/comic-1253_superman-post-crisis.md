# Superman (Post-Crisis)

Superman (Post-Crisis), real name Kal-El, is a good-aligned male Kryptonian character created by DC Comics, born in Krypton and first appearing in ACTION COMICS #1. He works as a Reporter for the Daily Planet and novelist and is affiliated with Justice League Elite, Superfriends, Justice Legion Alpha, Time Masters, Black Lantern Corps, Legion of Super-Heroes, Justice League, Justice League of America and Justice League Antarctica. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 191 cm tall and weighs 101 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Flight, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 95 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 100 for speed, 100 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 28.
