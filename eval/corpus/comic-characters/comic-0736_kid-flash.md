# Kid Flash

Kid Flash, real name Wallace Rudolph West, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Blue Valley, Nebraska and first appearing in Flash #110 (January, 1960). He is affiliated with Flash Family, Justice Legion Alpha, Black Lantern Corps and Teen Titans West. He has Green eyes and Red hair. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing and Super Speed. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 65 for intelligence, 5 for strength, 90 for speed, 40 for durability and 30 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
