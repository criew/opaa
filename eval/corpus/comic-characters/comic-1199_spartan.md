# Spartan

Spartan, real name John Diggle, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Starling City and first appearing in Arrow Season 1: Episode 1. He works as a Vigilante, Government Agent; formerly soldier, bodyguard. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 90 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 35 for speed, 45 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 6.
