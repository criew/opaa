# Kabuto Yakushi

Kabuto Yakushi, real name Yakushi Kabuto, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Shueisha, first appearing in Naruto, Volume #5, Chapter #39. He works as a Spy. He has Yellow eyes and Grey hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 64 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Energy Absorption, Enhanced Senses, Force Fields, Illusions, Intelligence, Mind Control, Reflexes, Regeneration, Shapeshifting, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 85 for speed, 80 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 15.
