# Skeletor

Skeletor, real name Keldor, is a bad-aligned male character created by Mattel, born in Snake Mountain, Infinita and first appearing in DC Comics Presents Vol. 1 #47 (July, 1982). He works as an Overlord of Infinita Would-Be-Conqueror. He has Red eyes and is bald. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Dimensional Travel, Electrokinesis, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Fire Control, Force Fields, Intelligence, Magic, Mind Control, Super Speed, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Telepathy Resistance, Teleportation, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 95 for strength, 100 for speed, 75 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 35.
