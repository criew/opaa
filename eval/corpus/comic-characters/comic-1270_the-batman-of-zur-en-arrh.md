# The Batman Of Zur-En-Arrh

The Batman Of Zur-En-Arrh, real name Tlano, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Zur En Arrh and first appearing in Batman #113 (August 1958). He works as a Superhero, Scientist. He has Blue eyes and Brown hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 95 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Endurance, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 45 for speed, 50 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 10.
