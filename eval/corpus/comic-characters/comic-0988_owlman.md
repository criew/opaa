# Owlman

Owlman, real name Thomas Wayne Jr., is a bad-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham (Earth 3) and first appearing in Justice League of America #29 - Crisis on Earth-Three!. He works as a Crimelord. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 95 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 50 for durability and 95 for combat, giving an overall score of 10.
