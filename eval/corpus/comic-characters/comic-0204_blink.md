# Blink

Blink, real name Clarice Ferguson, is a good-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in Bahamas and first appearing in Uncanny X-Men #317 (October, 1994). She works as an Adventurer, freedom fighter and is affiliated with The Hand, X-Men, New Mutants and Selene's Inner Circle. She has Green eyes and Magenta hair and stands 165 cm tall and weighs 56 kg. Her notable abilities include Teleportation. Rated across attributes, she scores 75 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 20 for speed, 30 for durability and 65 for combat, giving an overall score of 5.
