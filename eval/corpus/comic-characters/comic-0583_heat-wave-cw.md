# Heat Wave (CW)

Heat Wave (CW), real name Mick Rory, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Earth-1 and first appearing in The Flash Season 1: Episode 4. He works as an Adventurer, novelist; formerly criminal. He has Hazel eyes and No Hair hair and stands 185 cm tall and weighs 87 kg. His notable abilities include Durability, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Stamina, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 45 for durability and 85 for combat, giving an overall score of 4.
