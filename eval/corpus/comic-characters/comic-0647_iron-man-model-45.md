# Iron Man (Model 45)

Iron Man (Model 45), real name Tony Stark, is a good-aligned male Human character created by George Lucas, born in Long Island, New York and first appearing in Tales of Suspence #39 (March, 1963). He works as an Inventor. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 196 cm tall and weighs 189 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Element Control, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Energy Beams, Energy Blasts, Energy Manipulation, Flight, Force Fields, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Marksmanship and Reflexes. Rated across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 85 for strength, 70 for speed, 65 for durability and 70 for combat, giving an overall score of 16.
