# Scorpion

Scorpion, real name Hanzo Hasashi, is a neutral-aligned male character created by Mortal Kombat, born in Osaka Japan (Formerly), Netherealm (Reborn) and first appearing in Mortal Kombat. He works as a Ninja. He has White eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 95 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Element Control, Energy Blasts, Energy Constructs, Fire Control, Immortality, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Magic, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Teleportation. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 60 for speed, 60 for durability and 100 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 19.
