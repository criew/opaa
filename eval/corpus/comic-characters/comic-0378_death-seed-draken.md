# Death Seed Draken

Death Seed Draken, real name Akihiro, is a bad-aligned male Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, born in new york and first appearing in Wolverine: Origins #10. He has Blue eyes and Black hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 75 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Energy Absorption, Enhanced Senses, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Jump, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Regeneration, Stamina, Super Strength, Toxin and Disease Resistance and Weapon-Based Powers. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 30 for strength, 85 for speed, 100 for durability and 90 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 13.
