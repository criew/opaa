# T-850

T-850, real name Cyberdyne Systems Series 850 Terminator, is a bad-aligned male Cyborg character created by Dark Horse Comics, first appearing in Terminator 3: Rise of the Machines (2003). He works as an Assassin and is affiliated with Skynet. He has Red eyes and weighs 198 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Cold Resistance, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Longevity, Stamina, Stealth, Super Strength and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 90 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 25 for speed, 90 for durability and 75 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 9.
