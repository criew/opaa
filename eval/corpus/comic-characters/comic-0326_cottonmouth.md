# Cottonmouth

Cottonmouth, real name Burchell Clemens, is a bad-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Captain America #310 (October, 1985). He works as a Professional criminal and is affiliated with Serpent Society and A.R.M.O.R.. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 183 cm tall and weighs 99 kg. His notable abilities include Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 10 for speed, 10 for durability and 25 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 4.
