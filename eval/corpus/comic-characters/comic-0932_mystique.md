# Mystique

Mystique, real name Raven Darkholme, is a bad-aligned female Mutant character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in (as Raven Darkholme) MS. MARVEL #16, (as Mystique) MS. MARVEL #18. She works as a Special operative for Germany and the United States governments, (former) German Freedom Fighter, special operative in the Weapon X Project Team and is affiliated with The Hand, Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, Brotherhood of Mutants, Cerebro's X-Men, Hellfire Club, X-Men, Dark X-Men and Marauders. She has Yellow (without irises) eyes and Red / Orange hair and stands 178 cm tall and weighs 54 kg. Her notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Enhanced Senses, Longevity, Marksmanship, Reflexes, Shapeshifting, Super Speed, Super Strength, Telepathy Resistance and Toxin and Disease Resistance. Rated across attributes, she scores 85 for intelligence, 20 for strength, 50 for speed, 50 for durability and 90 for combat, giving an overall score of 7.
