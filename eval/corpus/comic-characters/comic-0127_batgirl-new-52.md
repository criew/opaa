# Batgirl (New 52)

Batgirl (New 52), real name Barbara Gordon, is a good-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Flashpoint Vol 2 #5. She works as a Vigilante, Businesswoman. She has Green eyes and Red hair and stands 170 cm tall and weighs 61 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stealth, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 100 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 25 for speed, 30 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 9.
