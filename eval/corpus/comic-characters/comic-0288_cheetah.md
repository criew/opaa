# Cheetah

Cheetah, real name Priscilla Rich, is a bad-aligned female Human character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Wonder Woman Vol1 #6 (Fall 1943). She is affiliated with Villainy, Inc. She has Green eyes and Blond hair and stands 163 cm tall and weighs 50 kg. Her notable abilities include Agility, Reflexes, Stamina and Stealth. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 70 for intelligence, 10 for strength, 40 for speed, 20 for durability and 70 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 4.
