# Abomination

Abomination, real name Emil Blonsky, is a bad-aligned male Human / Radiation character created by Marvel Comics, born in Zagreb, Yugoslavia and first appearing in Tales to Astonish #90. He works as an Ex-Spy and is affiliated with Annihilators, Wrecking Crew, Masters of Evil, Superiour Six, New Enforcers, Secret Empire and The Mighty Avengers. He has Green eyes and is bald and stands 203 cm tall and weighs 441 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Endurance, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Intelligence, Invulnerability, Jump, Marksmanship, Mind Control Resistance, Regeneration, Stamina, Stealth, Super Speed and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 85 for intelligence, 100 for strength, 80 for speed, 90 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 22.
