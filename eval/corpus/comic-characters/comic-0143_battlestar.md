# Battlestar

Battlestar, real name Lemar Hoskins, is a good-aligned male character created by Marvel Comics, born in Chicago, Illinois and first appearing in Captain America #323 (1986); (as Bucky). He works as a Former wrestler, federal operative. He has Brown eyes and Black hair and stands 198 cm tall and weighs 133 kg.
