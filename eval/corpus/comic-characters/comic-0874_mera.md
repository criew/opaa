# Mera

Mera, real name Mera, is a good-aligned female Atlantean character created by DC Comics, first appearing in Aquaman #11 (October, 1963). She works as a Former queen of Atlantis; Former queen of Dimension Aqua and is affiliated with Aquaman Family and Red Lantern Corps. She has Blue eyes and Red hair and stands 175 cm tall and weighs 72 kg. Her notable abilities include Cold Resistance, Dimensional Travel, Durability, Enhanced Hearing, Jump, Reflexes, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength and Telepathy. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, she scores 80 for intelligence, 60 for strength, 80 for speed, 60 for durability and 80 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, her overall score is 8.
