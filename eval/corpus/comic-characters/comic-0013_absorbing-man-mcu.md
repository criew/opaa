# Absorbing Man (MCU)

Absorbing Man (MCU), real name Carl Creel, is a good-aligned male Human character created by Marvel Comics, first appearing in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Season 2: Episode 1. He works as a Government Agent, Bodyguard. He has Brown eyes and is bald and stands 193 cm tall. His notable abilities include Agility, Cold Resistance, Durability, Energy Absorption, Fire Resistance, Heat Resistance, Invulnerability, Stamina and Super Strength. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 75 for intelligence, 15 for strength, 30 for speed, 40 for durability and 85 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 8.
