# Cole

Cole, real name Cole, is a male Human character created by Lego, born in Ninjago and first appearing in Way of the ninja. He works as a Royal Blacksmith (formerly)/ Elemental master of earth(actually). He has Black eyes and Black hair and stands 157 cm tall and weighs 45 kg. His notable abilities include Accelerated Healing, Agility, Durability, Element Control, Endurance, Enhanced Senses, Flight, Force Fields, Matter Manipulation, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stealth, Super Speed, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated across attributes, he scores 70 for intelligence, 50 for strength, 50 for speed, 80 for durability and 100 for combat, giving an overall score of 13.
