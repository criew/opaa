# Battle-Suit Batman (DCEU)

Battle-Suit Batman (DCEU), real name Bruce Wayne, is a good-aligned male Human character created by DC Comics, born in Gotham City and first appearing in Batman v. Superman - Dawn of Justice. He works as a Hero. He has White eyes and Black hair and stands 188 cm tall and weighs 79 kg. His notable abilities include Agility, Durability, Intelligence, Marksmanship, Peak Human Condition, Reflexes, Stamina, Super Strength, Weapon-Based Powers and Weapons Master. Rated on a 0-100 scale across attributes, he scores 100 for intelligence, 80 for strength, 50 for speed, 75 for durability and 95 for combat. On a separate overall ranking scale, his overall score is 12.
